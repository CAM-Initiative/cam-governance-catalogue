VIGIL Data Explorer

An experimental interactive view of the VIGIL Observatory corpus for examining taxonomy mappings, failure and invariant-held evidence, Incident severity, structured-data coverage and selected Harm Impact Assessment dimensions.

The interactive explorer reports its denominator and treats Incident-level harm as associated evidence rather than attributing harm to a taxonomy class.
