German television personality alleged deepfake pornography was distributed using her likeness

Independent reporting describes alleged non-consensual sexual deepfakes using German television personality Collien Fernandes' likeness and records death threats, police protection, a criminal complaint, large public demonstrations and pressure for legal reform. Fernandes alleged that her former husband distributed the material; he denied the allegation. The harm assessment concerns the evidenced dignitary consequences and does not decide the disputed perpetrator attribution.
