German television personality alleged deepfake pornography was distributed using her likeness

Sexual deepfake imagery using German television personality Collien Fernandes’ likeness circulated and prompted her complaint, threats, police protection, public demonstrations and debate over German law. Fernandes alleged her former husband Christian Ulmen distributed material; he denied responsibility. In May 2026 a Hamburg court, deciding an interim press-law application against Der Spiegel, found sufficient evidential basis to report the suspicion that he distributed others’ deepfake videos, but said the article did not imply he made them. The ruling did not determine criminal responsibility. The generating service, source-to-output chain and ultimate authorship remain unestablished in this Case File.
