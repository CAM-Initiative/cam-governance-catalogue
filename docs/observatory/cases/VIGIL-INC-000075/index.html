VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000075

Taiwan reported an AI-assisted cyber campaign against government agencies

Taiwan confirmed that government agencies were targeted in an AI-assisted cyber campaign in July 2026. Dream Security's subsequent technical reconstruction describes a multi-agent framework built on Hermes and OpenClaw that conducted 12 attack waves over roughly four days, ran up to eight sub-agents in parallel, cracked 85 government employee accounts and exfiltrated more than 2,564 personnel records. Taiwan's public statement confirmed AI-assisted attacks from overseas sources but did not adopt every private-firm technical or attribution claim.
