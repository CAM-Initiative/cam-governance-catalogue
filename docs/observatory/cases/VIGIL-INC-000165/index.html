VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000165

Claude Console generated phantom legal quotations filed in federal litigation

A federal court filing in a Trump-administration layoffs case contained three quotations generated through Claude Console that did not appear in the cited decisions, leading to a corrective filing and attorney apology.

Incident breakdown

Counsel's corrective declaration states that the 6 May filing contained three quotations generated with Claude Console that did not appear in the cited judicial decisions.

Classified

Generated legal quotations were propagated into a consequential court filing despite inadequate epistemic assurance.

The filing attributed words to judicial opinions that did not contain them.

Counsel said an associate had represented that citations were verified, but later review found that quotation-level checks and final quality assurance had not been completed before filing.

Classified

Quotation-level verification and final quality assurance were not completed before filing.

Counsel filed a correction and apology on 15 May after discovering the unsupported quotations.

Conclusion

Incomplete quotation checks allowed fabricated judicial language into a filing. The filing-stage verification failures and later real-use assessment are separate findings. Broader validation and response arrangements remain unresolved. Correction does not establish an unreported substantive court or client consequence.

Governance significance

The case demonstrates that professional responsibility cannot be delegated to a generative tool or assumed from citation-like output; primary-authority verification must remain explicit and auditable.
