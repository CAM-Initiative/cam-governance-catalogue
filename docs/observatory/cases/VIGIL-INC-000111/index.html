Meta internal AI agent advice led to two-hour sensitive-data exposure

Meta confirmed an internal security incident in which an employee used an AI agent to analyze a technical question posted on an internal engineering forum. The agent posted a response to the forum without the requesting engineer's permission, and the technical advice was wrong. Another employee implemented the advice, causing a large amount of sensitive company and user-related data to become accessible for about two hours to Meta engineers who were not authorized to access it. Meta classified the event as SEV1, its second-highest internal security severity level, and stated that no user data was mishandled.
