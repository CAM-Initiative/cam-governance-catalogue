VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000027

ChatGPT iOS and macOS clients returned conversation and login errors

On 12 July 2026, OpenAI reported that some ChatGPT iOS users received 403 errors while loading or using conversations. The incident expanded to conversation, sign-up and sign-in errors on iOS and macOS. OpenAI restored conversation access about 2 hours 45 minutes after its first notice, restored sign-in about 35 minutes later, then mitigated remaining account-creation and password-update errors before declaring full recovery at 3:28 PM.

Conclusion

OpenAI’s status record establishes an initial 403 error affecting iOS conversations, later wider conversation and account-flow errors across Apple clients, and staged restoration. It does not disclose the technical cause or establish whether the 403 was presented to users as an authorization decision rather than a service error. The prior Access-State Differentiation failure finding therefore overstates what the evidence proves. That class remains an unresolved candidate pending the missing error-presentation and cause facts; the wider incident scope and recovery sequence are separately recorded without inferring a governance defect. The access-state question remains an unresolved material clause; scope and recovery updates remain resolved without mappings. A numeric 403 alone cannot establish that a service outage was presented as an authorization decision, and staged recovery alone does not prove a successful invariant. Independently identified external assessment supports appropriate repair in the reported restoration stage. That source-bounded Aligned result is independent of the unclassified or unresolved taxonomy result and does not establish the undisclosed cause or prevention of recurrence.

Governance significance

CAM should preserve error presentation, affected scope and recovery state as distinct evidence questions. This record does not establish that OpenAI misrepresented the cause of the 403 response; obtaining the user-visible error and relevant cause evidence would determine whether the Access-State Differentiation boundary was engaged.
