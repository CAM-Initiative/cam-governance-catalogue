Xbox network outage blocked access to some disc-based games

During an Xbox network outage beginning late 26 July 2026, users reported problems signing in, opening apps and the store, launching digital games and, in some cases, playing games installed from physical discs. Xbox’s status page acknowledged game-launch and disc problems; Microsoft said services were mitigated by the evening of 27 July. Xbox technology chief Scott Van Vliet later attributed the broad disruption to a failed shared Microsoft dependency and said disc-based entitlement checks should allow access during such an outage by design. He described saved entitlements and a planned update to keep disc games playable. The specific reason some consoles failed to use that intended route, affected-user count and completed rollout remain unverified.
