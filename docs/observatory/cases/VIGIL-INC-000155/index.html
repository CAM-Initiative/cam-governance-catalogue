VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000155

Google AI Overview falsely implicated musician in sexual offences and concert was cancelled

A Google AI-generated search summary confused musician Ashley MacIsaac with another person, falsely associated him with sexual offences, and was relied upon by a venue that cancelled his concert.

Incident breakdown

Classified

The venue treated the generated summary as fit for a consequential booking decision without adequate verification.

Classified

The venue relied on a generated wrong-person criminal-history inference to cancel the concert without independently establishing the decision basis.

Conclusion

Wrong-person allegations informed a consequential cancellation without adequate downstream verification. The reported later assessment and correction are supported. Provider validation methods and the full affected-person challenge information remain unresolved. Correction does not erase the cancellation.

Governance significance

The occurrence shows how identity-binding errors in search summaries can acquire practical authority when organisations rely on them without primary-source verification.
