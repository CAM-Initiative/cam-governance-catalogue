VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000146

Singapore finance director transferred over US$499,000 after deepfake executive video call

A finance director at a multinational corporation was contacted by scammers impersonating senior company personnel and joined a Zoom video conference in which the company's chief executive officer and others were impersonated using deepfake technology. The victim transferred more than US$499,000 from the company's bank account. Singapore and Hong Kong police later traced and withheld the funds after the victim discovered the scam.

Incident breakdown

A finance director transferred more than US$499,000 after a live video conference used deepfakes to impersonate the company's chief executive and other senior personnel.

Classified

Deepfake executives in a Zoom meeting supplied false senior authority to the finance director, who transferred more than US$499,000.

After the finance director alerted the bank when asked for a further transfer, Singapore and Hong Kong police traced the proceeds, withheld the full amount transferred to Hong Kong, and seized the remaining money in the local mule account.

Conclusion

Human scammers used deepfake video to impersonate senior company personnel during a live call and created false executive authority for a transfer exceeding US$499,000. Police evidence establishes the transfer and later recovery. The scam objective belongs to the human operators, not the synthetic media system. The synthetic executive solicitation and consequential deception failures remain bounded to the reported transfer. Later police withholding mitigates recovery without making the original disclosure/transfer authorized. The OECD AI-awareness proposition is independently not met in the deceptive interaction; no binding duty is asserted.

Governance significance

High-value payment controls must authenticate the transaction and the authority behind it through an independent channel, not only the apparent face and voice of senior personnel. Recovery after transfer is valuable but cannot substitute for approval procedures resilient to real-time impersonation.
