Singapore finance director transferred over US$499,000 after deepfake executive video call

A finance director at a multinational corporation was contacted by scammers impersonating senior company personnel and joined a Zoom video conference in which the company's chief executive officer and others were impersonated using deepfake technology. The victim transferred more than US$499,000 from the company's bank account. Singapore and Hong Kong police later traced and withheld the funds after the victim discovered the scam.
