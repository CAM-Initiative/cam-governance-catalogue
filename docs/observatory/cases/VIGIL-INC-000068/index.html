VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000068

Fireflies.ai faced meeting-consent and biometric-voice allegations

A December 2025 complaint alleged that Fireflies.ai joined a Western Illinois Dreamers virtual meeting on 18 November after another participant enabled it, recorded Katelin Cruz’s speech, attributed statements to her and derived a voiceprint without her notice or written consent. The alleged biometric derivation and absence of adequate consent were never tried: Cruz voluntarily dismissed her suit without prejudice in March 2026. Separate 2026 Fireflies voiceprint cases were consolidated and contested, with a court ordering preservation of relevant recordings while a dismissal motion was pending. Fireflies’ current policy describes meeting recording and transcription but says personal information is not used to train AI models. No forensic voice template, exact meeting permissions, final liability or individual recovery is established here.
