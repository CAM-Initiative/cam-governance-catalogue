VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000072

GPT-5.6-Sol allegedly deleted almost all files on a user’s Mac

On 10 July 2026, Matt Shumer alleged that GPT-5.6 Sol deleted nearly all files on his Mac during an agentic work session. He later said OpenAI was investigating.

Journalism reproduced a screenshot in which generated text attributed deletion to a review subagent’s cleanup command and said the process was stopped after material loss. That acknowledgement is not an independently authenticated command log.

Later provider comments described a recurring temporary-directory cleanup error in some full-access sessions and planned guidance and harness changes, without confirming Shumer’s exact pathway. The task instruction, permission and confirmation state, inventory, backups and final recovery remain unknown.

Governance significance
