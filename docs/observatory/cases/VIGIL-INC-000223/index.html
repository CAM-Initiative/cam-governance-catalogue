VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000223

ContextEcho agentic-coding research released fabricated and misattributed bibliography entries

On 22 May 2026, researchers affiliated with Accenture's Center for Advanced AI released ContextEcho, an arXiv research preprint on assistant persona drift in long coding sessions. The original version contained bibliographic entries that misrepresented other research.

For example, reference 67 attributed a supposed 'Persona-driven sycophancy' paper to arXiv:2402.08471, an identifier that belongs to an unrelated physics article. A 4 November GPTZero investigation reported seven seriously incorrect references out of 102 assessed.

The research preprint was revised on 27 August and 29 October, but the source investigation does not establish what errors remain in each version. The publication-stage source fidelity problem is observable; any use of generative AI to compose particular references is inferred, not demonstrated by authoring traces or author admissions.

Conclusion

The assessed conduct is the release and research-stage reliance on specific unfaithful citations and claims within ContextEcho. The suspected participating generative-AI research/drafting component has not been identified; public output and independently checkable source discrepancies nevertheless show material below the epistemic assurance appropriate for publication.

The cause remains disputed or unverified: the resulting provisional Epistemic Reliance Calibration failure applies to the observable generated/retrieved/transformed/synthesised epistemic artefact as it was supplied for scholarly or policy reliance, not an assertion that any identified LLM generated the error. The authors and publishing bodies are accountable for publication, but the available evidence does not assign the erroneous words to a specific person, model or tool.

External GPTZero detection is not the assessed AI platform.

Governance significance

This occurrence illustrates the separation between observable deficient epistemic assurance and unknown internal generative-system topology. Professional reliance requires source verification proportionate to publication-stage evidentiary use even where model trace and authorship details are unavailable.

The correction or investigator response can be recorded without transferring its actions to the assessed synthetic component.
