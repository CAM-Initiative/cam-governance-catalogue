Hellobike robotaxi struck two pedestrians in Zhuzhou and service was suspended

On 6 December 2025, a Hellobike robotaxi collision in Zhuzhou injured two people. Reporting described bystanders and police lifting the vehicle to free a trapped person; hospital staff confirmed both injured people were taken for treatment. Hellobike suspended local autonomous-driving operations, and booking attempts returned out of service. The city’s service line confirmed the suspension on 15 December with no known resumption date. On 29 December traffic police said they had gathered evidence at Hellobike’s headquarters and commissioned a professional technical and safety inspection. No final fault determination, inspection result or exact driving/control state was established in the reviewed sources.
