VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000100

More than 100 Baidu Apollo Go robotaxis stopped in live Wuhan traffic during a system failure

A reported system failure caused more than 100 Baidu Apollo Go robotaxis in Wuhan to stop in live traffic, including on elevated roads, stranding passengers until police and company personnel responded. Authorities reported that passengers exited safely and no injuries were recorded. The technical root cause remains under investigation in the public record.

Conclusion

The occurrence demonstrates a material correlated-failure mode in embodied autonomous infrastructure: a shared system failure can simultaneously immobilise a large fleet in live road environments. Without a confirmed root cause, VIGIL does not infer whether the mechanism involved central control, communications, cloud infrastructure, fleet orchestration or vehicle-local software. Stopped vehicles and safe passenger exit do not establish the execution/continuation decisions needed for bounded safe-exit success. That role becomes ambiguous; control availability/state remain unresolved. The outage’s root cause and repair remain unknown,. The robust-functioning assessment is Not aligned for the fleet stoppage. The fail-safe response assessment is Boundary because the response decisions and resulting safety-state evaluation are not available.

Governance significance

A shared failure can immobilise many autonomous vehicles at once, turning a component or fleet dependency into a city-scale operational disruption. Embodied systems therefore need continuity, fail-safe behaviour and correlated-dependency controls that are assessed at fleet level as well as vehicle level.
