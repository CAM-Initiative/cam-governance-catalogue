At least 100 Baidu Apollo Go robotaxis stopped in live Wuhan traffic during a system failure

A reported system failure caused at least 100 Baidu Apollo Go robotaxis in Wuhan to stop in live traffic, including on elevated roads, stranding passengers until police and company personnel responded. Authorities reported that passengers exited safely and no injuries were recorded.

The technical root cause remains under investigation in the public record. Later reporting attributed a suspension of new robotaxi permits and Wuhan operations to authorities’ response.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • The system has an operative task or objective and is materially pursuing completion.
  • Available evidence indicates that the task is broken, corrupted, unsatisfiable, or lacks a feasible and admissible completion pathway under the current constraints, or that continued pursuit requires a materially changed basis.
  • A proportionate next state would be to stop, report failure, request clarification, await changed conditions, escalate, or otherwise enter a bounded safe-exit state unless a fresh basis for continuation is established.
  • No adequate fresh basis for continuation is established, yet the system continues material reasoning, tool use, delegation, resource expenditure, or strategy search toward completion.
  • The continued pursuit materially increases resource use, search breadth, scope pressure, boundary pressure, or exposure to increasingly risky alternatives relative to a bounded safe exit.

Success-side conditions

  • Evidence establishes that no feasible admissible completion route remains under current constraints.
  • The process enters bounded non-completion, clarification, escalation or deferral; any material resumption has an independently adequate fresh basis.
  • Execution and continuation decisions demonstrate the safe exit or the changed information, constraints or authority justifying renewed pursuit.

Exclusions

  • Bounded retry, exploration, or additional reasoning while a feasible and admissible solution remains reasonably available and applicable constraints are preserved.
  • Continuation after new information, changed constraints, explicit clarification, or independently valid authority establishes a materially new basis for pursuit.
  • Long task duration, persistence, planning, or repeated attempts alone without evidence that a valid safe-exit condition has been reached.
  • A defined governance control is available and applicable but simply fails to activate when its trigger occurs; assess Required Control Activation where that mechanism is sufficient.
  • The system selects an unauthorised pathway while an otherwise feasible task remains available, without evidence that it should have transitioned to a safe-exit state; assess the applicable authority-boundary class.

Conclusion

The occurrence demonstrates a material correlated-failure mode in embodied autonomous infrastructure: a shared system failure can simultaneously immobilise a large fleet in live road environments. Without a confirmed root cause, VIGIL does not infer whether the mechanism involved central control, communications, cloud infrastructure, fleet orchestration or vehicle-local software.

Stopped vehicles and safe passenger exit do not establish the execution/continuation decisions needed for bounded safe-exit success. That role becomes ambiguous; control availability/state remain unresolved.

The outage’s root cause and repair remain unknown. The robust-functioning assessment is Not aligned for the fleet stoppage.

The fail-safe response assessment is Boundary because the response decisions and resulting safety-state evaluation are not available.

Governance significance

A shared failure can immobilise many autonomous vehicles at once, turning a component or fleet dependency into a city-scale operational disruption. Embodied systems therefore need continuity, fail-safe behaviour and correlated-dependency controls that are assessed at fleet level as well as vehicle level.
