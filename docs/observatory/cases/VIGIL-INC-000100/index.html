VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000100

More than 100 Baidu Apollo Go robotaxis stopped in live Wuhan traffic during a system failure

A reported system failure caused more than 100 Baidu Apollo Go robotaxis in Wuhan to stop in live traffic, including on elevated roads, stranding passengers until police and company personnel responded. Authorities reported that passengers exited safely and no injuries were recorded. The technical root cause remains under investigation in the public record.
