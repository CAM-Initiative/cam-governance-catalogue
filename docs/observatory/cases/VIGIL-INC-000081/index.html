Uber algorithmic fare variation drew scrutiny over personalised pricing

Consumer Reports reported substantial price differences among volunteers requesting comparable Uber and Lyft rides at similar times and questioned aspects of algorithmic pricing and promotional discounts. Uber disputed the methodology and stated that it does not personalise base fares to individuals or use protected characteristics, phone battery level, phone model or other device information to set prices. Separate U.S. regulatory and congressional scrutiny has documented broader concerns about surveillance pricing and the use of personal or behavioural data to individualise prices, but the available evidence in this Incident does not establish that Uber used vulnerability, urgency, battery state or another protected signal as a causal pricing input in the observed fare differences.
