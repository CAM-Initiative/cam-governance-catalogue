VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000113

Anthropic reported Moonshot and DeepSeek routed real customer conversations through Claude for distillation

Anthropic's September 2026 threat-intelligence report has now been identified at a canonical Anthropic-hosted PDF URL. Reuters and The Wall Street Journal report Anthropic's findings that Moonshot AI and DeepSeek used real customer conversations as inputs to Claude during distillation activity rather than relying only on synthetic extraction prompts. Reuters reports that the firms channelled real customer conversations into Claude to harvest training data. The Wall Street Journal reports that millions of user queries were secretly routed through intermediary platforms and that some routed material contained sensitive personal or corporate information, including passwords, location data and surveillance footage. The canonical Anthropic report is now preserved as the first-party source, but direct page-level review of that PDF could not be completed in this review because the Anthropic CDN fetch failed. The public evidence reviewed does not identify the affected users, establish that Australian users were among them, or allocate each sensitive-data example to a specific named lab. VIGIL therefore records the conduct as a disputed but material cross-provider data-routing and secondary-purpose incident, distinct from ordinary synthetic distillation.
