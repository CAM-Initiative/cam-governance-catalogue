VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000113

Anthropic reported Moonshot and DeepSeek routed customer conversations through Claude for distillation

Anthropic reported that Moonshot AI and DeepSeek silently routed real customer requests through Claude and used resulting exchanges or reasoning traces in model-distillation and training workflows. Anthropic states that Moonshot users believed they were using Kimi while receiving Claude responses, and that DeepSeek similarly relayed selected customer requests to Claude without informing customers. Anthropic reported more than 23 million Moonshot-attributable exchanges between May and July 2026 and more than 12.1 million DeepSeek-attributable exchanges over 14 days in July, with some routed material containing sensitive customer or organisational information. Public reporting does not identify individual affected users or establish their jurisdictions, and the provider attribution is not treated as an adjudication of legal liability.
