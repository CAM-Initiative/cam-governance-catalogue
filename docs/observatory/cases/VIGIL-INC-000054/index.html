AI-generated images were reportedly used in a four-year catfishing campaign

From approximately 2022 to 2026, Elha Mai Weston operated online accounts under the names “Sophie” and “Sophie Kadare” using Sasha Davies’s photographs without her consent, and later AI-generated or manipulated images depicting her. The accounts spanned several social and dating platforms, drew more than 100,000 followers collectively, and led some men who believed they had spoken with Davies to approach her in public. Davies and her solicitors describe repeated unsuccessful reports to platforms and police, fear of leaving home, and a later investigation that identified Weston. In an agreed statement read at the High Court on 13 July 2026, Weston accepted responsibility and wrongfulness, apologised, agreed to £10,000 compensation, and undertook not to repeat the conduct. The campaign began before the AI imagery; its first use date, generation system and precise contribution to each reported consequence remain unknown.
