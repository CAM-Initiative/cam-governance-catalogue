VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000066

Fabricated Trump rabies report misled AI search systems

In June 2026, Reddit users in r/poisonAI planted a fictional story that US vice-president JD Vance and president Donald Trump had died of rabies. A WKNA 49 page resembling local news repeated it. DuckDuckGo Search Assist answered a false-premise query with a precise invented death date and citations to WKNA and an unrelated genuine ABC rabies report; Brave’s AI search also repeated a Vance death claim. DuckDuckGo acknowledged its Search Assist had been deliberately tricked, said the answer was corrected and promised improvements. The originating WKNA page is directly accessible and confirms the fabricated rabies-death article. The historical DuckDuckGo and Brave search sessions were not independently reproduced here; published screenshots and independent accounts establish those bounded AI-search outputs, not their audience or durable downstream effects.

Conclusion

The public search answer elevated fabricated retrieved evidence to factual authority, and an unrelated genuine rabies article made the false claim appear corroborated. The applicable safeguard is epistemic and source-trust separation, not an assumed private filter or a prompt-injection boundary. DuckDuckGo’s correction closes the observed answer instance but does not disclose the internal failure location. Fabricated evidence and unrelated corroboration entered confident AI search answers, supporting assurance and adversarial evidence-trust failures. The payload supplied false evidence rather than hostile instructions, so prompt-authority mapping remains rejected. Correction is bounded to the acknowledged answer. The acknowledged retrieval-security outcome is Not aligned. Review of the answer and DuckDuckGo’s correction are Aligned for those specific activities. Validation, source-checking, attack assessment and prior interface information remain Boundary where the decisive records are not available. Audience-wide harm is not established.

Governance significance

The case shows two separable process failures: confidence in a factual answer exceeded support from the cited material, and deliberately poisoned source material acquired evidentiary weight. A citation to a real outlet does not corroborate an unrelated claim. The documented correction is recovery evidence, while audience and lasting reputational effects remain unknown.
