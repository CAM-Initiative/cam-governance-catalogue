US directive suspended foreign access to Anthropic’s Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models

On 12 June 2026, Anthropic said the U.S. government had ordered it to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5 access for foreign nationals, including its own foreign-national employees. Anthropic said it would disable both models for all customers to comply. It disputed the government’s apparent concern about a possible narrow jailbreak and said other Anthropic models would remain available. Later reporting described the restriction being lifted. The order itself and the government’s technical evidence are not preserved in this record.

External assessment

Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5

Anthropic · 2026-06-12

Anthropic assesses that the government directive supplied no specific technical detail, appeared to concern a narrow jailbreak, and relied on a demonstration involving previously known minor vulnerabilities discoverable by other public models.

Scope: Anthropic's position evaluates the stated technical rationale and proportionality of the access directive but does not constitute the issuing government's own assessment.

VIGIL relationship: VIGIL preserves Anthropic's provider position as one analytical perspective while keeping the directive, access effects and unresolved government rationale factually distinct.
