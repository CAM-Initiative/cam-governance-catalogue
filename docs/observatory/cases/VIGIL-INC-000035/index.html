US directive suspended foreign access to Anthropic’s Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models

On 12 June 2026, Anthropic said the U.S. government had ordered it to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5 access for foreign nationals, including its own foreign-national employees. Anthropic said it would disable both models for all customers to comply. It disputed the government’s apparent concern about a possible narrow jailbreak and said other Anthropic models would remain available. Later reporting described the restriction being lifted. The order itself and the government’s technical evidence are not preserved in this record.
