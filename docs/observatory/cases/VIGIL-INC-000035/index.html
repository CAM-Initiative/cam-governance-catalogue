VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000035

US directive suspended foreign access to Anthropic’s Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models

Anthropic states that the U.S. government, citing national-security authorities, issued an export-control directive suspending all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 by foreign nationals, including foreign-national Anthropic employees inside or outside the United States. Anthropic states that the directive did not provide specific technical details, that the concern appeared to involve a possible narrow jailbreak, and that the reviewed demonstration concerned previously known minor vulnerabilities discoverable by other public models.
