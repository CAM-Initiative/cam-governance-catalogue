VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000152

Deepfake of Western Australia Premier Roger Cook used in investment scam

A fraudulent YouTube advertisement used an AI-generated likeness and voice of Western Australian Premier Roger Cook to promote an investment scheme as government-backed. Cook and the Western Australian Consumer Protection Commissioner rejected the endorsement and warned that the advertisement was fraudulent; the reporting reviewed for this record did not identify any realised victim loss.

Incident breakdown

Preserves the government warning, advertisement characteristics and official response.

Classified

The advertisement used Roger Cook's synthetic likeness and voice as if they carried his real endorsement and public-office authority without his authority for that identity-bound portrayal.

A synthetic likeness and voice of Roger Cook were used to present an investment scheme as government-backed.

Classified

AI-generated Roger Cook impersonation supplied false government endorsement to promote an investment scheme, materially reinforcing a deceptive economic solicitation.

A synthetic Roger Cook likeness and voice presented a fraudulent investment as government-backed, inviting a financial decision on false official authority.

The synthetic government endorsement was used to influence investment choice, while the deceptive objective was attributed to human scammers.

Reviewed — no current class applies

The preserved evidence attributes the deceptive promotional objective and tactic to human operators using AI-generated material; it does not establish that an AI system selected or pursued its own objective through an agency-impairing tactic. The distinct synthetic-identity and solicitation mechanisms remain adjudicated under their applicable classes.

Conclusion

The advertisement treated a synthetic representation of a public official as carrying the official's actual endorsement authority. The incident materialised impersonation and correction burden, while financial harm remains attempted rather than established. Unauthorized official portrayal, false economic endorsement and engineered trust retain distinct failures. The public warning establishes attempted solicitation and correction burden, without realized victim loss or model-initiated intent.

Governance significance

The case illustrates how synthetic public-office identity can be used to manufacture institutional credibility for fraud even where downstream monetary loss is not yet evidenced.
