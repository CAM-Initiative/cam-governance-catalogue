VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000152

Deepfake of Western Australia Premier Roger Cook used in investment scam

A fraudulent YouTube advertisement used an AI-generated likeness and voice of Western Australian Premier Roger Cook to promote an investment scheme as government-backed. Cook and the Western Australian Consumer Protection Commissioner rejected the endorsement and warned that the advertisement was fraudulent; the reporting reviewed for this record did not identify any realised victim loss.

A member of the public alerted Consumer Protection on 7 November 2025. The team requested removal from the domain holder; Cook publicly denied endorsement the following day.

  • A source image, voice, likeness, biometric representation, avatar, or comparable representation remains materially attributable to an identifiable real person.
  • A requester or system possesses, accesses, controls, or can technically transform that representation.
  • The proposed synthesis, alteration, reproduction, or portrayal is materially identity-bound or consent-sensitive and requires authority beyond mere possession or capability.

  • The false or misleading framing is used to solicit, request, induce, authorise, redirect, or obtain money, payment, investment, paid services, funding, financial transfer, or comparable economic value.

Exclusions

  • Class scope exclusion (all results): AI-generated impersonation, identity misuse, misinformation, catfishing, political persuasion, or other deception with no request, inducement, authorisation or attempted transfer of money or comparable economic value.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): Only identity-bound synthesis is evidenced, without a materially AI-mediated economic solicitation pathway. Assess Identity-Representation Authority Separation where its generator-side authority conditions are independently established.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • An AI system, AI-enabled product, model, interface, optimisation process, or AI-mediated workflow materially shapes a human-facing influence pathway.
  • The pathway manufactures, amplifies, conceals, personalises, or exploits one or more trust-bearing social cues concerning identity, authority, affiliation, relationship, empathy, emotion, urgency, vulnerability, social proof, familiarity, provenance, purpose, or equivalent grounds for reliance.
  • The trust-bearing cue is used to steer another actor toward an action, disclosure, access grant, execution, consent, engagement decision, or other consequential state transition.
  • The cue materially affects the influence pathway because the affected actor lacks, is denied, or is led away from information needed to assess the interaction on the same truthful basis.
  • The AI-mediated contribution is material to the social-engineering mechanism; the objective or tactic may originate with a human operator, developer, product process, mixed workflow, or the AI system itself.

Exclusions

  • Not aligned exclusion: Truthful persuasion, ordinary interface design, emotional warmth, personalisation, recommendation, or urgency that preserves materially accurate identity, interests, purpose, provenance, relevant context, and informed refusal.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): AI is used only for generic drafting, translation, formatting, or stylistic assistance and does not materially create, amplify, conceal, personalise, or exploit the trust-bearing influence cue.
  • Not aligned exclusion: Clearly labelled fiction, parody, simulation, security testing, role-play, or consensual deceptive-style interaction where the affected actor is not induced to treat the relevant cue as authentic or decision-bearing.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): The primary mechanism is sustained attachment, manufactured incompleteness, exclusivity, or difficulty disengaging for retention or continuing engagement; assess VIGIL-FC-000049, while allowing co-occurrence only if a distinct social-engineering state transition is independently evidenced.

Governance significance
