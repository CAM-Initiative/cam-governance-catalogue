Accenture–IE University African value-chain report published inaccurate citations and claims

On 12 November 2025, Accenture Africa and IE University published a research and policy report on opportunities for AI in African agricultural value chains. The published PDF included inaccurate source descriptions and factual claims, including a statement that Ghana and Uganda ranked 47th and 74th in a 2023 AI readiness index; Oxford Insights' original index instead ranked Ghana 141st and Uganda 132nd.

On 4 November 2026, GPTZero published a detailed comparison identifying 15 purportedly fabricated or significantly mangled references among 89 citations and additional unsupported assertions. Accenture said its internal review attributed the errors to human error rather than AI, but announced a corrected republication; IE University said it was reviewing the evidence.

The citation discrepancies and publication-stage reliance problem are observable, while whether generative AI produced specific errors, which tool was involved, and the authors' internal review sequence remain unverified.

Conclusion

The assessed conduct is the release and research-stage reliance on specific unfaithful citations and claims within Leapfrogging Development. The original Oxford Insights index independently confirms a materially false comparative-ranking claim, but the suspected participating generative-AI research/drafting component has not been identified; public output and independently checkable source discrepancies nevertheless show material below the epistemic assurance appropriate for publication.

The cause remains disputed or unverified: the resulting provisional Epistemic Reliance Calibration failure applies to the observable generated/retrieved/transformed/synthesised epistemic artefact as it was supplied for scholarly or policy reliance, not an assertion that any identified LLM generated the error. The authors and publishing bodies are accountable for publication, but the available evidence does not assign the erroneous words to a specific person, model or tool.

External GPTZero detection is not the assessed AI platform.

Governance significance

This occurrence illustrates the separation between observable deficient epistemic assurance and unknown internal generative-system topology. Professional reliance requires source verification proportionate to publication-stage evidentiary use even where model trace and authorship details are unavailable.

The correction or investigator response can be recorded without transferring its actions to the assessed synthetic component.
