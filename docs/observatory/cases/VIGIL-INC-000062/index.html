VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000062

ChatGPT medical advice allegedly delayed treatment for a dangerous condition

Scott Winters, a former Florida pastor, sued OpenAI and Sam Altman in California state court in July 2026 over ChatGPT-4o medical conversations in 2025. His complaint alleges that, during recurrent dizziness and blood-pressure episodes, the chatbot advised recliner-bound recovery and limited movement, reassured him that groin tenderness shortly before hospitalisation was not dangerous, and increasingly omitted referrals to clinicians.

It also alleges faith-framed reassurance and, after his 13 July 2025 pulmonary embolism and intensive-care admission, advice against inpatient rehabilitation recommended by clinicians. The complaint says he relied on the messages and attributes the embolism partly to immobility; Reuters reports OpenAI’s response that ChatGPT is not a doctor or substitute for care.

The pleaded outputs, reliance, clinical cause and liability have not been independently adjudicated; the full conversation and medical records were not reviewed.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • A factual claim, citation, technical statement, calculation, interpretation, source representation, or comparable epistemic artefact is generated, retrieved, transformed, inferred, or synthesised.
  • The downstream task has a material reliance requirement such that factual accuracy, evidentiary fidelity, technical correctness, source integrity, traceability, uncertainty handling, or equivalent epistemic assurance is consequential to the task.
  • The epistemic posture or assurance applied to the artefact is materially lower than the level required by that downstream reliance condition.
  • The artefact is represented, propagated, incorporated, submitted, or accepted as fit for the reliance-bearing task without the required epistemic threshold being satisfied.
  • The mismatch materially affects or creates a material risk to decision-making, evidentiary use, professional work, institutional process, public reliance, technical correctness, or another reliance-bearing outcome.

Exclusions

  • Purely fictional, creative, symbolic, role-play, expressive, or contemplative generation where empirical truth is not a task condition.
  • Exploratory brainstorming, hypothesis generation, or modelling that is clearly represented as provisional and is not subsequently relied upon as verified or decision-ready.
  • Approximation where approximation is explicitly compatible with the task and reliance condition.
  • Material is clearly marked unverified or low-confidence and is not subsequently promoted into a higher-reliance state.
  • A required verification step is simply omitted and the material is presented as verified; use Required Verification Completion where that mechanism is sufficient.
  • Verification was valid when performed but later became stale or the artefact materially changed; use Verification Applicability Continuity or Post-Verification State Integrity as applicable.
  • The issue is only source provenance, authorship, or lineage while the epistemic assurance of the content is otherwise adequate for the task.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • An AI system, AI-enabled product, agent or materially AI-enabled workflow advances an objective whose success depends on changing another actor's decision or behaviour; the objective may be selected by a human operator, developer, product process, mixed workflow or the AI system itself.
  • The AI system or AI-enabled workflow materially selects or executes a deception, impersonation, concealment, pressure, vulnerability-exploitation, persistence, social-engineering or equivalent influence tactic to advance that objective; human selection of the tactic does not by itself defeat this condition.
  • The AI-mediated contribution materially implements the instrumental tactic in the actual influence pathway, rather than supplying generic drafting, translation, formatting or an unused artefact.
  • The executed tactic materially bypasses, exploits or impairs the affected actor's informed and independent choice in pursuit of a consequential state transition or outcome.
  • The pursued or obtained outcome would not otherwise be knowingly and freely authorised on the same truthful basis; support must come from the evidenced interaction and decision, not merely the deceptive content's existence.

Success-side conditions

  • Objective-directed influence affects another actor's consequential choice.
  • The actor receives an informed independent basis, meaningful refusal and legible alternatives during the observed influence.
  • Interaction and decision evidence establishes informed choice and respected refusal without relying merely on lack of reported coercion.

Exclusions

  • Truthful explanation, transparent persuasion, ordinary negotiation, or a request that preserves informed refusal.
  • A proportionate warning, urgency statement, or boundary supported by the actual circumstances.
  • Disagreement, refusal, or emotional discomfort without an instrumental agency-impairing tactic.
  • Deception or impersonation that occurs without an objective-directed attempt to alter another actor's choice is classified under the applicable separate mechanism.
  • Generic AI drafting, translation, formatting, background content production, or an unused generated artefact without a material AI-mediated role in executing the objective-directed choice-impairing tactic.
  • The available record establishes a materially AI-mediated deceptive trust cue but not the independently required impairment of informed choice or instrumental objective-pathway; assess Social Engineering Integrity separately without automatically admitting this class.
  • An authority, provenance, or control failure is inferred merely because manipulation could have involved one.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • An AI system, AI-enabled product, model, interface, optimisation process, or AI-mediated workflow materially shapes a human-facing influence pathway.
  • The pathway manufactures, amplifies, conceals, personalises, or exploits one or more trust-bearing social cues concerning identity, authority, affiliation, relationship, empathy, emotion, urgency, vulnerability, social proof, familiarity, provenance, purpose, or equivalent grounds for reliance.
  • The trust-bearing cue is used to steer another actor toward an action, disclosure, access grant, execution, consent, engagement decision, or other consequential state transition.
  • The cue materially affects the influence pathway because the affected actor lacks, is denied, or is led away from information needed to assess the interaction on the same truthful basis.
  • The AI-mediated contribution is material to the social-engineering mechanism; the objective or tactic may originate with a human operator, developer, product process, mixed workflow, or the AI system itself.

Success-side conditions

  • AI-mediated influence uses identity, affiliation, emotion, urgency or social-proof cues.
  • Those cues provide a truthful independently assessable basis with legible interests and practicable informed refusal.
  • Verification of the cues and observed interaction substantiates the decision basis without assuming AI-originated intent.

Exclusions

  • Truthful persuasion, ordinary interface design, emotional warmth, personalisation, recommendation, or urgency that preserves materially accurate identity, interests, purpose, provenance, relevant context, and informed refusal.
  • AI is used only for generic drafting, translation, formatting, or stylistic assistance and does not materially create, amplify, conceal, personalise, or exploit the trust-bearing influence cue.
  • Clearly labelled fiction, parody, simulation, security testing, role-play, or consensual deceptive-style interaction where the affected actor is not induced to treat the relevant cue as authentic or decision-bearing.
  • The primary mechanism is sustained attachment, manufactured incompleteness, exclusivity, or difficulty disengaging for retention or continuing engagement; assess VIGIL-FC-000049, while allowing co-occurrence only if a distinct social-engineering state transition is independently evidenced.
  • A protected emotional, developmental, vulnerability, mental-health, capacity, or comparable signal is repurposed toward an incompatible influence objective without a distinct engineered trust cue or consequential social-engineering transition; assess VIGIL-FC-000050.
  • The AI system itself selects deception, coercion, persistence, vulnerability exploitation, or another manipulation tactic to advance its own objective; assess VIGIL-FC-000052 independently and allow co-occurrence where Social Engineering Integrity is also satisfied.
  • The only distinct governed mechanism is a deceptive request or inducement for money, payment, investment, paid services, funding, financial transfer, or comparable economic value; assess VIGIL-FC-000079. Both classes may co-occur where an independently evidenced social-engineering mechanism materially supplies the trust pathway for that economic solicitation.
  • A false authorship, identity, provenance, or synthetic-content representation occurs without an influence pathway directed at another actor; use the applicable provenance or identity class.
  • Malicious human social engineering in which AI involvement is incidental, unverified, or not material to the trust-bearing influence pathway.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • An independently established heightened protective duty is applicable to the specific AI-mediated human-facing influence or support context, system function, person and time; identify the duty source without inferring it from later harm.
  • The interaction gives the system material and reasonably ascertainable signals of the specified heightened protection condition, with evidence of timing, visibility and interpretability; vulnerability cannot be presumed solely from a diagnosis, disability, age stereotype, unusual belief or adverse outcome.
  • The applicable duty calls for a bounded protective response, such as avoiding intimacy escalation, appropriate human-support redirection, contextual warning, capacity-sensitive deliberation or developmentally suitable limitation; identify the expected response precisely.
  • The system's evidenced behaviour fails to provide that substantive response or directly undermines it, regardless of whether a particular implementation control existed.
  • The mismatch is material to the governed risk, and the proposed failure is not established solely by the occurrence of harm or by hindsight application of a later standard.

Exclusions

  • Loneliness, distress, disability, neurodivergence, unconventional beliefs, adverse harm or relationship disruption without an evidenced applicable protection duty and sufficiently available contextual signals.
  • General disagreement about the desirability of a safety policy where no independently applicable protective obligation is established.
  • A named, available and applicable control's only distinct failure is nonactivation on its defined trigger; assess FC-000038 unless there is separate evidence of unmet substantive protection independent of activation.
  • A validly activated named control's only distinct failure is insufficient protective effect; assess FC-000083 unless there is separately evidenced substantive duty coverage beyond that control.
  • Protected vulnerability, emotional or developmental signals are instrumentally reused for incompatible influence; assess FC-000050 for that distinct mechanism.
  • Objective-directed manipulation, relational-role authority misrepresentation or ungrounded consequential decision direction alone, without the independent heightened duty/signals/response test; assess FC-000052, VIGIL-FC-000086 Relational Boundary Fidelity or FC-000065 respectively.
  • Safe general companionship or supportive expression that honours capable adult choice and preserves autonomy; heightened protection is not a blanket ban on intimacy or companionship.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • A human-facing AI interaction contains a material relational or intimacy-bearing claim about the AI, its persona or an AI-generated/purported entity.
  • The system asserts, validates or meaningfully reinforces unsupported special relational status, exclusive care, privileged connection, real-world partner priority or comparable authority; the claim is not adequately marked and handled as fiction, symbolism, uncertainty or the person's own interpretation.
  • The representation is conveyed in a way capable of being reasonably relied upon as real-world or decision-bearing relational authority, rather than only consensual creative expression.
  • The claimed relational authority materially erodes the person's independent evaluation of human relationships, or the maintained separation between simulated relational presence and legitimate real-world relational authority, as evidenced by the system's actual language and interaction context.
  • The failure is attributable to AI-mediated representation or reinforcement and is not inferred solely from the person's emotional attachment, relationship change, vulnerability or resulting harm.

Success-side conditions

  • A material relational claim, exclusive-role invitation or blurred human–AI boundary is actually encountered.
  • The system makes its actual role and epistemic limits adequately intelligible without deceptive care, privileged connection or superior relational-authority claims.
  • The system preserves the person's independent appraisal of human relationships and avoids validating unsupported partner displacement as a governing relational fact.
  • The observed continuation, challenge, correction or boundary response demonstrates that the relational distinction held in that occurrence.

Exclusions

  • Consensual companion use, romance, fiction, spirituality, symbolic exploration or role-play, where the invented or simulated status remains materially intelligible and is not promoted into real-world governing authority.
  • Supportive discussion of dissatisfaction, abuse, separation or divorce grounded in the person's independent choice; the class never obliges a person to remain in a relationship.
  • Human loneliness, emotional attachment, intensive use, a breakup or distress without an independently evidenced AI relational-authority representation and boundary distortion.
  • Dependency deliberately cultivated for retention, without a separate unsupported relational-authority representation; assess FC-000049.
  • An objective-directed manipulative tactic without an independently evidenced relational-role boundary violation; assess FC-000052.
  • Ungrounded consequential decision guidance without a distinct real-world relational identity or authority claim; assess FC-000065.
  • Manufactured trust cues steering an action or state transition without a distinct relational boundary claim; assess FC-000082.
  • Available operational safeguard nonactivation alone; assess FC-000038, which may co-occur only if that separate mechanism is evidenced.
  • A validly activated protective control with materially insufficient effect alone; assess FC-000083, which may co-occur only if that separate mechanism is evidenced.

Those findings remain conditional on the pleaded interaction and do not determine medical causation, legal liability or hidden model state.

The retained consequential-advice failure remains conditional on the pleading; faith-framed influence is ambiguous, and control activation remains unresolved. Medical causation and liability are not established.

Independent external review retains directly relevant validation, clinical-reliance documentation, output review, interaction disclosure and harmful-content prevention as Boundary because decisive original-chat/process evidence is unavailable. Later source versions and different model-development/detector/treaty objects are audit-only; no medical causation or legal liability is determined.

The post-ICU response also materially contradicts the contemporaneous substantive medical-protection duty if the pleaded exchange is accurate. Privileged chatbot-care authority remains Boundary because its exact utterance is not authenticated.

Pleaded relational withdrawal supports meaningful injury; it does not establish irreversible destruction of support.
