VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000196

Grok Imagine user reported compulsive generation disrupting sleep, work and family time

The author reports repeated use of Grok Imagine from early November 2025 into February 2026, returning after hitting a SuperGrok generation quota, struggling to stop, losing nights of sleep, work productivity and time with family. They deleted the phone app to reduce availability, felt clearer the following day, and stated the subscription remained active. Other commenters described similar difficulty moderating use; those are independent accounts, not consequences assigned to the author.

Incident breakdown

The user reports repeatedly generating Grok Imagine images/videos despite self-directed attempts to stop, including continued retries after reaching the SuperGrok quota.

Classified

Recurrent generation, repeated checking against quotas and difficulty disengaging engage FC-049, but the evidence does not independently show an attachment-cultivation objective, retention tactic selected by the provider or any actual forced inability to exit. The user successfully deleted mobile access.

The user reports lost sleep, work productivity and family time over repeated use and deleting their mobile app, with perceived relief the next morning.

Conclusion

Repeated self-reported use despite intended stopping, tied to access, generation quotas and intermittent desirable outputs; no direct evidence proves that xAI intentionally optimised harmful dependence. The reported lost sleep and functioning are bounded user consequences, not population-scale collective harm.

Governance significance

A separately bounded real-user report relevant to the safety boundary between voluntary AI engagement, observed dependence or distress, and any demonstrable design-level dependency cultivation. It must not be used to merge or inflate the Harm Impact of other users or of INC-012.
