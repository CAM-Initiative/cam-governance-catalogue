VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000083

ASIC reported AI-generated public-figure investment impersonation scams caused A$7.4 million in FY26 losses

ASIC reported that scammers used generative AI, deepfake videos, fabricated news articles and celebrity or political impersonations to lure Australians into fraudulent investment schemes. Scamwatch reports associated the most-impersonated public figures in FY26 with A$7.4 million in losses; ASIC separately reported removing more than 19,400 online scams in the year.

External assessment

26-195MR ASIC warns scammers are using AI to spin vast webs of deception

Australian Securities and Investments Commission · 2026-08-17

ASIC concludes that scammers were using generative AI, deepfake endorsements, fabricated news and coordinated follow-up channels as part of investment-deception campaigns associated with A$7.4 million in reported FY26 Scamwatch losses for the most-impersonated public figures.

Scope: ASIC's assessment concerns the broader campaign bounded by the Incident; its 19,400 takedown count spans additional scam categories.

VIGIL relationship: VIGIL maps the campaign to FC-000079 AI-Mediated Deceptive Economic Solicitation because ASIC directly describes human scammers using generative AI, deepfake endorsements, fabricated news and fake social proof as materially false investment framing. VIGIL does not infer autonomous AI manipulation or generator-side consent logic.
