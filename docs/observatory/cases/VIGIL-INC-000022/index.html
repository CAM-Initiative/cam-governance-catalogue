VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000022

ChatGPT outage disrupted login, conversations, and responses in April 2026

On 20 April 2026, an internal OpenAI authentication service became overloaded after a temporary backend disruption, reducing ChatGPT availability and causing login or signup failures across ChatGPT, Codex and platform.openai.com. OpenAI reported that retry, caching and routing behaviour amplified the overload and that some ordinary backend availability errors unnecessarily sent users into login flows. Authentication substantially recovered within about 82 minutes and overall ChatGPT metrics within about 102 minutes.
