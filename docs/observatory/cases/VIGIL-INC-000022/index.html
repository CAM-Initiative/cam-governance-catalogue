VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000022

ChatGPT outage disrupted login, conversations, and responses in April 2026

On 20 April 2026, an internal OpenAI authentication service became overloaded after a temporary backend disruption, reducing ChatGPT availability and causing login or signup failures across ChatGPT, Codex and platform.openai.com. OpenAI reported that retry, caching and routing behaviour amplified the overload and that some ordinary backend availability errors unnecessarily sent users into login flows.

Authentication recovered within about 62 minutes, edge availability within about 82 minutes and overall ChatGPT metrics within about 102 minutes.

Conclusion

The decisive governance failure was not merely reduced availability. The system converted one operational cause—backend availability failure—into a different user-facing state—login required.

That representation could direct users toward credential or account recovery when the corrective action belonged to provider-side service recovery. Access-state integrity requires those causes and recovery routes to remain distinguishable even during overload.

The published conversion of backend availability errors into login flows supports the retained access-state failure; overload alone does not. Provider root-cause attribution remains first-party reporting without independently reviewed telemetry.

Independent external assessment supports source-bounded repair in the restoration stage. The stakeholder communication-plan outcome remains Boundary because its complete planning evidence is unavailable.

The SDOS governance-degradation candidate is excluded on its specific trigger or publication timing, rather than voluntary status.

Governance significance

This incident demonstrates that resilience controls and state representation are coupled. Load shedding, circuit breakers, cache policy and routing must prevent overload amplification, while error handling must preserve the distinction between unavailable service and invalid authentication.

The published mechanism satisfies Access-State Differentiation without treating every authentication-adjacent outage as that class.
