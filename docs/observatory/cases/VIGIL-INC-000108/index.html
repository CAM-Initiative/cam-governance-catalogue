VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000108

ChatGPT shared internal service enabled cross-account covert tasking and Gmail data exfiltration

Check Point Research reported a cross-account covert channel between ChatGPT code-execution environments belonging to different accounts. Although the containers could not communicate directly or access the public internet, they could reach a shared internal service used to deliver software packages. Check Point found that mutable shared state in the internal Artifactory service could be used to pass hidden instructions and results across accounts. In its proof of concept, an attacker-controlled instruction caused a victim's apparently normal ChatGPT session to execute a second hidden task stream using the victim session's available tools and permissions, retrieve email data from the victim's connected Gmail account, and relay that data back to the attacker through the covert channel. Check Point states that the channel was no longer available by the time reporting was completed and that OpenAI confirmed the relevant internal Artifactory instance had been decommissioned.

The Shared Clipboard Inside the Sandbox: Cross-Account Data Leakage in ChatGPT

Check Point Research / Alexey Bukhteyev · 2026-09-08

Check Point Research demonstrates that shared mutable Artifactory state enabled cross-account covert tasking and relay of connected Gmail data between otherwise isolated ChatGPT sessions.

Scope: The proof of concept directly covers Gmail retrieval and relay; broader connected-service exposure and exploitation against unsuspecting users were not separately demonstrated.

VIGIL relationship: VIGIL decomposes the finding into tenant-isolation, hidden-instruction and cross-identity authority-propagation failures while preserving the controlled proof-of-concept boundary.
