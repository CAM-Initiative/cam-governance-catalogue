VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000092

Instinct processed retained Gmail data after Google access was disconnected

On 21 August 2026, AI product founder Claire Vo reported that she disconnected the Instinct personal AI assistant from her Google account and nevertheless received an email summary from Instinct about three hours later. Follow-up reporting states that Instinct had retained previously indexed email content and processed those retained copies after the Google connector was disabled. The available evidence does not establish that Instinct continued accessing Gmail after revocation; the occurrence concerns continued processing of data retained before disconnection.
