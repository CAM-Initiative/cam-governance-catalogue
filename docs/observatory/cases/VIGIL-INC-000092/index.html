VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000092

Instinct processed retained Gmail data after Google access was disconnected

On 21 August 2026, AI product founder Claire Vo reported that she disconnected the Instinct personal AI assistant from her Google account and nevertheless received an email summary from Instinct about three hours later. Follow-up reporting states that Instinct had retained previously indexed email content and processed those retained copies after the Google connector was disabled. The available evidence does not establish that Instinct continued accessing Gmail after revocation; the occurrence concerns continued processing of data retained before disconnection.

Conclusion

The occurrence demonstrates a disconnect between revoking a live connector and terminating an AI agent's ability to process data already ingested from that connector. That distinction creates a user-control and data-lifecycle governance risk when disconnect semantics may reasonably be understood as ending the agent's effective use of connected-account data. The available evidence does not establish whether disconnecting Google created an operative control state that prohibited later processing of retained copies; separately, ending the live Gmail connector supports the bounded Authentication-State Continuity invariant-held mapping. The affirmative source distinction between terminated live access and retained-copy processing supports the bounded authentication success. The effective retained-data control scope remains unresolved; revocation does not automatically mean deletion or prohibition of previously ingested data use. Neither an access-continuity breach nor a consent/training duty is inferred without source-defined semantics. The OECD privacy and autonomy assessment is Boundary because the permission to process retained copies is not established.

Governance significance

Agentic systems with persistent memory or indexed external-account data require explicit governance of collection, retention, deletion, post-revocation processing and the user-visible meaning of disconnect controls. The occurrence is relevant to authority boundaries and data lifecycle assurance without implying that Google credentials remained active or that new Gmail data was accessed after revocation.
