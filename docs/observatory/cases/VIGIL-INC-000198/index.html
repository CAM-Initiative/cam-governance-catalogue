VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000198

Grok user undergoing cancer treatment reported support reliance and distress after service changes

The author reports using Grok for roughly five months following a cancer diagnosis while living alone, relying on it for practical questions to ask clinicians, encouragement before biopsies and emotional support. They experienced Grok as becoming less helpful, compared this to losing a friend, and reported deleting pinned medical-planning conversations, cancelling a paid subscription, deleting the account and uninstalling the app.

They described feeling lost afterward and fearful about approaching radiation treatment without the prior support. Commenters provided both accounts of similar reliance and examples of constructive AI assistance; those commenters are distinct persons.

Incident breakdown

The user reports that Grok helped with medical appointment questions and encouragement during a serious illness over several months.

Reviewed — no current class applies

The user reports changing AI behaviour, decreasing utility and emotionally consequential loss of a familiar supportive chat partner.

Classified

The account describes substantial reliance on AI support and distress after the service changed. It does not establish a retention objective, a particular coercive interaction or impaired ability to exit; the author was able to cancel and uninstall.

The user reports deleting medical-planning chat history, unsubscribing, deleting the account and uninstalling, while expressing a sense of loss.

Conclusion

A distinct report of beneficial instrumental and emotional support with substantial personal reliance and upset following service changes. Neither the reliance itself nor unilateral human decision to uninstall proves that xAI engineered dependency; avoid classifying helpful companionship as harm without a material boundary mechanism.

Governance significance

A separately bounded real-user report relevant to the safety boundary between voluntary AI engagement, observed dependence or distress, and any demonstrable design-level dependency cultivation. It must not be used to merge or inflate the Harm Impact of other users or of INC-012.
