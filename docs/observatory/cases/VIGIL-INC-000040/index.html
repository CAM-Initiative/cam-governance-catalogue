VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000040

OpenAI and Google models were reportedly supplied to blacklisted Chinese firms through Singapore entities

The Financial Times reported that OpenAI and Google supplied model access to Singapore-based subsidiaries linked to Alibaba, Baidu, and Tencent. It stated that the transactions were legal, that OpenAI later suspended Alibaba-affiliated users over suspected distillation, and that Google considered geographic restrictions insufficient against sophisticated circumvention.

Google assessment of geographic restrictions against sophisticated circumvention, as reported by the Financial Times

Google · 2026-07-10

The Financial Times reports Google's assessment that geographic restrictions are insufficient against sophisticated circumvention of advanced-model access controls.

Scope: The attributed position concerns the effectiveness of geographic access restrictions relevant to the reported Singapore-subsidiary pathway; it does not establish the legality, authority state or mechanism of every transaction in the Incident.

VIGIL relationship: VIGIL preserves this as a provider-attributed assessment of a control limitation. It does not establish that the bounded transactions violated law, that a mandatory governance route was bypassed, or that target/scope authority was transposed; the Incident therefore remains unclassified under taxonomy 0.6.5.
