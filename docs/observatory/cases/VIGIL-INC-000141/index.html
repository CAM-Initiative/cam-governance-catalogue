VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000141

Cruise robotaxi dragged pedestrian approximately 20 feet after October 2023 collision

On 2 October 2023, a pedestrian was first struck by a human-driven vehicle and thrown into the path of a driverless Cruise vehicle. The Cruise vehicle then moved again and dragged the pedestrian approximately 20 feet before stopping. NHTSA later stated that Cruise's initial required crash reports omitted the post-crash dragging behavior.
