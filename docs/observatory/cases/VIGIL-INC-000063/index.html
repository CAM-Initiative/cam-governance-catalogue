Google AI reportedly produced inaccurate cancer guidance

Independent reporting found that Google AI Overviews produced inaccurate and potentially harmful health guidance, including cancer-related treatment advice and misleading interpretation of liver-function results. Medical experts warned that the confident presentation could deter users from seeking appropriate care. No specific resulting clinical injury is established in this Incident.
