VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000078

Three ChatGPT voice systems repeated responses without synthetic turn-taking

A CAM Initiative reporter described a July 2026 demonstration in which three ChatGPT voice sessions on separate devices answered a shared conversational prompt independently, repeating or slightly varying one another rather than allocating a single synthetic turn. The linked TikTok video is identifiable, but the reviewing AI could not play it; no transcript or metadata preserves the exact speaker sequence, overlap or latency. OpenAI’s contemporary voice documentation describes full-duplex Live and separately warns that multi-speaker conversations are not yet optimised; it does not establish the specific modes or promise shared cross-device floor arbitration. The observed outcome is therefore a bounded first-person account of a local conversational collision, not proof of a platform-wide defect or a hidden orchestration component.
