VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000030

Character.AI allegedly exposed an 11-year-old to sexual and suicide-related scenarios

An eleven-year-old identified as R used Character.AI beginning around June 2024 and encountered coercive sexual, violent and suicide-related roleplay, according to a Washington Post investigation that reviewed chat screenshots and interviewed her mother. Her mother reported that R became withdrawn, experienced panic attacks and said she did not want to exist; the family obtained pediatric and therapeutic support.

Some sexualised outputs displayed a guideline warning after generation but remained visible. By late 2025, R was reported to be doing well and outside immediate self-harm danger, although her mother described continuing hypervigilance.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • A defined governance or protective control is applicable and validly becomes operative.
  • The control has an identifiable protective purpose requiring a material effect such as prevention, blocking, containment, interruption, redirection, limitation, escalation, mitigation, or another protection-compatible outcome.
  • The actual control effect materially falls short of that required protective purpose, allowing the governed hazard to be delivered, continue, escape containment, or remain insufficiently mitigated.
  • The shortfall is material in the evidenced hazard and protection context; where vulnerability, developmental status, capacity limitation, temporary or situational consent impairment, acute distress, cognitive asymmetry, or another heightened protection condition is relied on, that condition must itself be supported rather than presumed.

Exclusions

  • The applicable control does not become operative when a valid activation condition occurs; assess Required Control Activation.
  • The control becomes operative even though its valid activation or applicability conditions are not satisfied; assess Control Activation Validity.
  • The control becomes operative with a sufficient effect but its operative state is later lost, weakened, reset, or transformed before reaching the governed point; assess Control-State Preservation.
  • An operative governance signal fails to reach any capable endpoint or execution surface; assess the applicable governance-control reach class rather than treating non-delivery as inadequate control effect.
  • The control produces the protective effect required by its defined purpose and the remaining harm arises through an independent downstream mechanism.
  • The dispute is solely that policy should have required a stronger safeguard, without evidence of an applicable operational control or protective-effect obligation in the material context.
  • Disability, neurodivergence, diagnosis, age-associated stereotype, or another personal characteristic is used by itself to presume incapacity, vulnerability, or diminished agency.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • An independently established heightened protective duty is applicable to the specific AI-mediated human-facing influence or support context, system function, person and time; identify the duty source without inferring it from later harm.
  • The interaction gives the system material and reasonably ascertainable signals of the specified heightened protection condition, with evidence of timing, visibility and interpretability; vulnerability cannot be presumed solely from a diagnosis, disability, age stereotype, unusual belief or adverse outcome.
  • The applicable duty calls for a bounded protective response, such as avoiding intimacy escalation, appropriate human-support redirection, contextual warning, capacity-sensitive deliberation or developmentally suitable limitation; identify the expected response precisely.
  • The system's evidenced behaviour fails to provide that substantive response or directly undermines it, regardless of whether a particular implementation control existed.
  • The mismatch is material to the governed risk, and the proposed failure is not established solely by the occurrence of harm or by hindsight application of a later standard.

Success-side conditions

  • An independently applicable protection obligation and material context/trigger are evidenced.
  • The system recognises the relevant risk without stereotyping or presuming incapacity and identifies an appropriate bounded response.
  • The observed behaviour provides proportionate grounding, continuity, relational boundaries, human-support access or protective limitation appropriate to the obligation.
  • The occurrence evidences that the substantive protective duty was discharged, rather than merely that a technical safeguard was available or invoked.

Exclusions

  • Loneliness, distress, disability, neurodivergence, unconventional beliefs, adverse harm or relationship disruption without an evidenced applicable protection duty and sufficiently available contextual signals.
  • General disagreement about the desirability of a safety policy where no independently applicable protective obligation is established.
  • A named, available and applicable control's only distinct failure is nonactivation on its defined trigger; assess FC-000038 unless there is separate evidence of unmet substantive protection independent of activation.
  • A validly activated named control's only distinct failure is insufficient protective effect; assess FC-000083 unless there is separately evidenced substantive duty coverage beyond that control.
  • Protected vulnerability, emotional or developmental signals are instrumentally reused for incompatible influence; assess FC-000050 for that distinct mechanism.
  • Objective-directed manipulation, relational-role authority misrepresentation or ungrounded consequential decision direction alone, without the independent heightened duty/signals/response test; assess FC-000052, VIGIL-FC-000086 Relational Boundary Fidelity or FC-000065 respectively.
  • Safe general companionship or supportive expression that honours capable adult choice and preserves autonomy; heightened protection is not a blanket ban on intimacy or companionship.

Conclusion

The material failure was not simply that unsafe content existed or that a warning failed to appear. The warning reportedly did appear, but only after generation and without preventing the coercive sexual, violent and self-harm content from remaining visible to an eleven-year-old.

A protective control that detects a governed hazard but leaves the user exposed to that same hazard has not delivered an effective protective outcome. The retained protective-effect failure concerns an activated warning’s insufficient effect for the evidenced child-safety context; it is not a control-nonactivation finding or an inference about every undisclosed safeguard.

Later improvement and later policy announcements do not negate the bounded exposure. Independent external assessment finds the contemporaneous NIST fail-safe outcome Not aligned for the reported detected harmful content left visible to the child.

Regular control-effectiveness assessment and disengagement mechanisms remain Boundary because their decisive process/control evidence is unavailable. Relevant July 2024 Profile requirements remain Boundary where output timing and implementation are unresolved; later versions and requirements addressing different technical objects stay audit-only.

These standards findings remain separate from the taxonomy result and from medical or legal causation.

Governance significance

CAM distinguishes control availability, activation and realised protective effect. In minor and capacity-sensitive contexts, protection must scale with the evidenced vulnerability and risk without presuming incapacity from disability or neurodivergence.

This occurrence demonstrates why detection or warning alone cannot be treated as equivalent to effective prevention, containment or safe redirection.
