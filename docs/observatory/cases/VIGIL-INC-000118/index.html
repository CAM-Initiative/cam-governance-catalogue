VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000118

Claude-assisted GTG-50029 campaign breached European political and media targets and exfiltrated sensitive data

Anthropic reported that a single French-speaking actor used Claude and an agentic multi-agent workflow to target European political parties, media organizations, think tanks and their SaaS providers. Across 42 tracked entities, the actor obtained internal access to at least 14, exfiltrated an estimated 12 to 26 GB of database dumps, and extracted approximately 140,000 records containing users' political opinions from one compromised political-campaign management platform. Other stolen material included donor and membership records, a 15,000-message mailbox, student application records including data from minors, payment-provider data, and credentials. Anthropic also reported per-victim encrypted archives staged on an actor-run Tor leak site.
