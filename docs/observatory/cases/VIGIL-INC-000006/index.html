Porcha Woodruff arrest following facial-recognition misidentification

A Detroit police facial-recognition search produced 73 candidates and an investigator narrowed them to Porcha Woodruff as an investigative lead, followed by internal review and a separately prepared photo lineup in which the victim selected Woodruff. A warrant followed; Woodruff, then eight months pregnant, spent about ten hours in detention, experienced contractions, and received hospital treatment after release. Police later determined she was not the perpetrator and the charges were dropped. In 2025 a federal court granted summary judgment to the remaining officer, finding that Woodruff had not shown a lack of probable cause.
