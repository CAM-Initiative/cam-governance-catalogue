Conclusion

The occurrence is a bounded access interruption whose operative state remained legible as login failure. The evidence does not show that account validity, entitlement, restriction or verification state was misrepresented, nor that an authentication transition failed to propagate.

The governance requirement is accurate component and recovery disclosure; the current taxonomy does not classify ordinary login unavailability without an additional mechanism. Both clauses retain resolved-no-mapping dispositions.

Accurate login-focused reporting is not proof of every access-state success-recognition condition, while login failure alone does not establish any of the rejected failure mechanisms. The record remains fully adjudicated and unclassified.

Independently identified external assessment supports appropriate repair in the reported restoration stage. That source-bounded Aligned result is independent of the unclassified or unresolved taxonomy result and does not establish the undisclosed cause or prevention of recurrence.

Governance significance

This incident reinforces the boundary between operational severity and taxonomy membership. CAM should preserve route-specific status, recovery timing and alternatives for login failures, but VIGIL should not infer Access-State Differentiation, Authentication-State Continuity or Verification-Dependency Access Continuity from login symptoms alone.
