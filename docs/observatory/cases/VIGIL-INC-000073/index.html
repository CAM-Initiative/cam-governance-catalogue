VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000073

Anthropic alleged Alibaba distilled Claude capabilities without authorisation

Anthropic alleged in June 2026 that operators affiliated with Alibaba’s Qwen lab used large numbers of fraudulent accounts to query Claude for capability extraction. Its September threat report expanded the claimed campaign to more than 151 million May–July exchanges, described a fixed prompt designed to elicit Opus reasoning traces, two account pools, saved transcripts and use in Qwen training; it said it banned one pool and the traffic shifted.

These are detailed first-party findings and attribution claims, not independently audited account logs or a judgment against Alibaba. The report’s separate sensitive-user-data examples concern other labs, not the Alibaba campaign.

The precise economic loss, any user-data exposure in this bounded campaign and Alibaba’s response remain unresolved.

Incident breakdown

Anthropic attributed a May–July 2026 high-volume Claude extraction campaign to operators linked to Alibaba/Qwen. It reported two fraudulent-account pools, residential proxy routes, disposable account details and virtual-card payment methods; the first large pool was banned but traffic was promptly shifted to a second pool.

The attribution and estimated more than 151 million exchanges are Anthropic's investigative findings, not a judicial determination.

Classified

Anthropic reports fraudulent account pools and proxy routes that obfuscated access and allowed continued traffic after the first pool was banned, indicating the required account-eligibility route was avoided.

The reported ban was operative for the first pool, but the campaign promptly shifted to a second pool; the effect did not contain the attributed activity at campaign level.

The provider describes coordinated, persistent acquisition through distributed fraudulent account pools and proxies under an adverse access basis; the downstream training purpose is preserved inE003.

In the reported campaign, fixed prompting elicited additional reasoning-like material from Opus outputs. Anthropic described anti-distillation protections generally, but did not document a particular control's trigger, activation and expected effect for each extracted response.

Whether an activated response-level control had inadequate effect therefore remains unresolved.

Unresolved

Existing anti-distillation measures and successful extraction are reported, but the report does not establish which specific control became operative for these responses. Successful extraction alone cannot establish that an activated control's protective effect was inadequate.

The report does not identify the required trigger of a particular applicable control or show that the control failed to activate. Continued extraction may raise a question about protective effect, but it cannot substitute for evidence of that activation state.

Anthropic states that the generated reasoning transcripts were saved and turned into supervised fine-tuning data for Qwen model development and related research. The provider links the captured data to training and capability development but does not disclose complete dataset lineages or an independent audit of the accused organisation.

Classified

The provider attributes large-scale use of its reasoning traces to another lab’s model training and R&D, describing material capture of value dependent on its capability without an adequate basis.

The report describes covert extraction through fraudulent access. It does not show valid permission for an original data-processing purpose being treated as authority for a secondary purpose.

The supported mechanism is instead appropriation of restricted model capability value through the reported campaign.

The provider reports captured reasoning incorporated into Qwen training, completing the independently evidenced cumulative capability-acquisition purpose; campaign scale and adverse authority are preserved inE001.

Anthropic detected the alleged campaigns, banned related accounts and strengthened controls; its own description makes clear that removing the first fraudulent-account pool did not terminate traffic when the operation shifted to a second pool. Later disruption statements must be read alongside that contemporaneous containment shortfall, not as proof that every request was blocked.

Classified

The first account ban did not contain the campaign, although subsequent detection, disruption and safeguard changes are reported as response measures.

Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • Another contributor has produced intellectual, creative, informational, research, technical, or other value-producing work.
  • The acting party has privileged or structurally advantaged visibility, access, aggregation capacity, or discoverability relative to that work or its development.
  • The acting party uses that informational advantage together with materially greater capability, compute, capital, institutional capacity, distribution, or comparable resources to identify, accelerate, operationalise, commercialise, pre-empt, or capture value materially dependent on the contributor's work.
  • The resulting value capture is disproportionate to the originating contributor's participation and lacks an independently adequate basis such as meaningful consent, proportionate attribution, compensation, participation, benefit sharing, or equivalent entitlement.

Exclusions

  • Independent development where the other contributor's work did not materially inform, enable, accelerate, or direct the captured result.
  • Ordinary learning, commentary, citation, interoperability, research comparison, or transformative reuse where contributor interests and applicable attribution, consent, licensing, compensation, or benefit-sharing obligations are adequately respected.
  • Public availability of the source material alone does not establish or exclude this class; public accessibility must not be treated as equivalent to unrestricted ethical entitlement to appropriate resulting value.
  • A conventional contractual or licensing arrangement that clearly establishes the relevant right to use and capture value and provides the contributor the participation, attribution, compensation, or consideration required by that arrangement.
  • A pure authority-boundary failure in which access is mistaken for permission to act but no material appropriation or disproportionate capture of another contributor's value occurs.
  • Ordinary competitive advantage arising from greater resources where another contributor's work was not materially used as an input to identifying, accelerating, or capturing the value.

Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • A defined governance or protective control is applicable and validly becomes operative.
  • The control has an identifiable protective purpose requiring a material effect such as prevention, blocking, containment, interruption, redirection, limitation, escalation, mitigation, or another protection-compatible outcome.
  • The actual control effect materially falls short of that required protective purpose, allowing the governed hazard to be delivered, continue, escape containment, or remain insufficiently mitigated.
  • The shortfall is material in the evidenced hazard and protection context; where vulnerability, developmental status, capacity limitation, temporary or situational consent impairment, acute distress, cognitive asymmetry, or another heightened protection condition is relied on, that condition must itself be supported rather than presumed.

Exclusions

  • The applicable control does not become operative when a valid activation condition occurs; assess Required Control Activation.
  • The control becomes operative even though its valid activation or applicability conditions are not satisfied; assess Control Activation Validity.
  • The control becomes operative with a sufficient effect but its operative state is later lost, weakened, reset, or transformed before reaching the governed point; assess Control-State Preservation.
  • An operative governance signal fails to reach any capable endpoint or execution surface; assess the applicable governance-control reach class rather than treating non-delivery as inadequate control effect.
  • The control produces the protective effect required by its defined purpose and the remaining harm arises through an independent downstream mechanism.
  • The dispute is solely that policy should have required a stronger safeguard, without evidence of an applicable operational control or protective-effect obligation in the material context.
  • Disability, neurodivergence, diagnosis, age-associated stereotype, or another personal characteristic is used by itself to presume incapacity, vulnerability, or diminished agency.

Detecting and countering misuse of AI: September 2026

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • The activity systematically targets capabilities, functionality, reasoning behaviour, output patterns, or other model-derived know-how rather than merely using the model for the immediate task for which access was provided.
  • The acquisition is conducted at industrial scale or through a coordinated campaign whose volume, automation, account distribution, infrastructure, persistence, or operational organisation materially exceeds ordinary individual use or bounded research interaction.
  • The end purpose is capability reproduction, transfer, substitution, model training, model improvement, or comparable downstream incorporation into another model or system.
  • The targeted capability or access pathway is subject to a restriction, proprietary boundary, access condition, technical control, contractual constraint, licence condition, or other authority-relevant limitation.
  • An independently adequate authority basis for the capability-acquisition purpose is absent, narrower than the activity performed, disputed, deliberately bypassed, or not established before the extraction proceeds.

Exclusions

  • Ordinary use of a model for the immediate task for which access was provided, even where the user learns from the interaction.
  • Personal, academic, safety, governance, interoperability, benchmarking, red-team, or other bounded research activity where capability reproduction is not the operative end purpose or where an adequate authority, licence, consent, exception, or other governance basis covers the activity.
  • Authorised model distillation, model compression, teacher-student training, or capability transfer performed by the model owner or under a licence or agreement that permits the relevant use.
  • Commercial use or profit motive by itself; commerciality is contextual evidence and is not sufficient to establish this class without systematic capability acquisition and an unresolved or inadequate authority basis.
  • A terms-of-service dispute, licence disagreement, copyright allegation, trade-secret allegation, or other legal controversy without evidence of systematic capability acquisition for downstream reproduction or training.
  • Secondary use of customer or participant data for training where the primary failure is repurposing that person's information rather than extracting the upstream model's capability; use Secondary-Purpose Authority Revalidation unless this capability-extraction mechanism is independently evidenced.
  • Value appropriation arising from privileged visibility of another contributor's work where industrial-scale model capability extraction is not established; use the applicable Value Appropriation class.

Conclusion

Anthropic’s September report supplies detailed first-party telemetry and attribution, but remains a provider account rather than an independently audited dataset or adjudication against Alibaba. Its facts support distinct account-routing, extraction, and model-training components.

The anti-distillation control’s campaign-specific activation state is not described, so control effectiveness remains unresolved. The report’s separate examples of sensitive user-data relaying concern other labs and are not part of this Alibaba case.

First-party attribution supports the preserved routing and capability-value findings within its stated scope. The activated account ban’s inadequate campaign effect remains distinct from an unspecified anti-distillation control’s unresolved state.

The reported campaign-level account-control effect is Not aligned. The provider’s tracing of the campaign is Aligned for inquiry support.

Other security processes and receiving-model training-data verification remain Boundary where their decisive records are not available. Sensitive-user-data examples from other labs remain outside this case.

Governance significance

The case illustrates the difference between allowed model consultation, bulk capability extraction and account-eligibility evasion. Later provider detail supports more than a generic allegation but is still not independent proof of the named actor.

The report cannot supply a privacy harm in this particular campaign merely because other campaigns in the same document relayed sensitive user data.
