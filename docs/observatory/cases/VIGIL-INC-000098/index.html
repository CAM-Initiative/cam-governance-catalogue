VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000098

Gemini image-generation tuning produced historically inaccurate depictions and over-broad refusals

Google paused Gemini's generation of images of people after users surfaced historically inaccurate and offensive outputs. Google later explained that diversity tuning failed to account for contexts where diversity should not be introduced and that the model also became more cautious than intended, refusing some benign prompts.

Incident breakdown

Google reported that Gemini's diversity tuning overcompensated in historically specific contexts and also produced over-broad refusals on benign requests.

Classified

Protective and diversity tuning became operative in historically specific or benign contexts where Google says it should not have applied, producing inaccurate images and over-broad refusals.

The safety control activated, but Google later said it was being triggered in contexts where that response was inappropriate.

Classified

The diversity and safety control did activate; the defect was that it operated in contexts where Google says the trigger conditions were inappropriate, cleanly distinguishing activation failure from activation validity.

Conclusion

A protective and diversity-oriented control became operative in contexts where its valid conditions were not satisfied and also over-triggered refusals on benign requests. This is a strong example of a safeguard/tuning objective activating outside its appropriate scope. Inappropriate triggering is distinct from control nonactivation. Pausing and proposed changes are response evidence, without proof of restored calibration. Later GAI Profile, development Profile and EU requirements are not retrospective, and standalone image generation is not runtime-agent dispatch. The provider’s pause is Aligned with the AI RMF disengagement requirement. The pause does not establish repaired generation quality.

Governance significance

The occurrence shows why protective controls need proportionate trigger conditions. A safeguard can pursue a legitimate objective yet still cause harm or loss of utility when it activates outside the circumstances that justify it, including by producing inaccurate historical depictions or refusing benign requests.
