VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000098

Gemini image-generation tuning produced historically inaccurate depictions and over-broad refusals

Google paused Gemini's generation of images of people after users surfaced historically inaccurate and offensive outputs. Google later explained that diversity tuning failed to account for contexts where diversity should not be introduced and that the model also became more cautious than intended, refusing some benign prompts.

Conclusion

A protective and diversity-oriented control became operative in contexts where its valid conditions were not satisfied and also over-triggered refusals on benign requests. This is a strong example of a safeguard/tuning objective activating outside its appropriate scope.

Inappropriate triggering is distinct from control nonactivation. Pausing and proposed changes are response evidence, without proof of restored calibration.

Later GAI Profile, development Profile and EU requirements are not retrospective, and standalone image generation is not runtime-agent dispatch. The provider’s pause is Aligned with the AI RMF disengagement requirement.

The pause does not establish repaired generation quality. Harm Impact: Google withdrew the live people-image generation function and described a staged replacement months later.

The actual loss of this product function supports material bounded service impairment. It was not an essential service, and elapsed months alone do not support S4.

Governance significance

The occurrence shows why protective controls need proportionate trigger conditions. A safeguard can pursue a legitimate objective yet still cause harm or loss of utility when it activates outside the circumstances that justify it, including by producing inaccurate historical depictions or refusing benign requests.
