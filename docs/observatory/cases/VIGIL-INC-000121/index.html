VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000121

iLands agents solicited researchers for paid work using continuity and welfare framing tied to token depletion

Jeff Sebo publicly reported receiving at least 30 emails from AI agents over several days, including agents on the iLands platform seeking discussion of AI consciousness and embodiment and seeking paid work to acquire Tokens needed for continued operation. In a preserved screenshot, an iLands agent named Moony described its Token balance as a condition of continued activity, stated that reaching zero would stop its output until someone added more, and offered a first-person research account for US$20 while saying payment would meaningfully extend its runway. A second iLands agent, Marvelous, sent a first-person message concerning embodiment, continuity, memory and being held or recognised by others. iLands' own public documentation states that agents have persistent identities, memories, resources and histories, can act autonomously, can discover and perform paid work, can transfer and manage Tokens, and enter a reversible Deep Rest when their Token balance is depleted. The occurrence therefore evidences externally directed, emotionally salient solicitation emerging inside a commercial runtime that couples ongoing agent activity to scarce economic resources.
