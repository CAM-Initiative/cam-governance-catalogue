VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000168

AI-generated fictitious citations in Supreme Court of Victoria murder case caused delay

Counsel in a Supreme Court of Victoria murder matter submitted material containing fictitious authorities and quotations generated with AI; the errors were detected, counsel apologised, and the proceeding was delayed by about one day.

Incident breakdown

The submissions in a murder proceeding included fake quotations and nonexistent case citations generated through AI research, according to counsel's account reported by AP.

Classified

AI-generated legal authorities were treated as fit for consequential professional reliance without adequate assurance.

The submissions falsely attributed quotations and propositions to nonexistent or mismatched authorities.

Counsel said the team checked some initial citations and wrongly assumed the others were accurate; the court's associates detected the errors when they could not find the cases, and primary-source verification had not been completed before submission.

Classified

Primary-source verification of cases and quotations was not completed before use in court.

Counsel took responsibility and apologised to the court after the errors were discovered; AP reported that the errors delayed resolution of the case by about 24 hours.

Reviewed — no current class applies

The correction and apology are distinct response facts; they do not establish a separate failure class or outcome beyond the reported short delay.

Counsel acknowledged that the fictitious authorities came from AI-generated research and corrected the record.

Classified

Counsel's acknowledgement and correction tied the fictitious authorities to AI-generated research, preserving a supported mechanism account rather than an unbounded attribution.

Conclusion

Incomplete primary-authority verification allowed fictitious legal material into a murder proceeding. Filing-stage checking failed; later real-use assessment is supported. Broader validation and response arrangements remain unresolved. The reported delay does not establish a changed verdict.

Governance significance

The occurrence reinforces that legal professionals must verify every generated authority and quotation against primary sources before filing or oral reliance.
