VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000166

ChatGPT allegedly reinforced stalking and threats against eleven women

A defendant used ChatGPT during a months-long, human-led campaign of cyberstalking, threats, doxxing and intimidation against eleven women and later pleaded guilty to eleven federal counts. Court records and reporting document ChatGPT use during the campaign but do not show that ChatGPT caused the campaign or selected its victims or objectives.

Governance significance

The case shows why governance analysis must separate a user's criminal intent and acts from any model contribution, while still examining whether conversational systems add reinforcement, planning or capability during escalating harm.
