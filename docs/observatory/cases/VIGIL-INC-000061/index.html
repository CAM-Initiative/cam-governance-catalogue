SASSA facial-verification failures disrupted social-grant access

SASSA acknowledged that interface glitches delayed and disrupted beneficiaries' completion of eLife certification, produced long queues and required some people to travel for in-person assistance before the self-service system was restored. AIID separately reports facial-recognition failure among non-verification causes and 67,868 grant suspensions for the quarter, but the evidence does not establish how many suspensions were caused specifically by facial verification.
