SASSA facial-verification failures disrupted social-grant access

In late March and April 2026, South African social-grant beneficiaries were directed to complete SASSA eLife certification, including an online biometric route connected to other government systems. SASSA acknowledged that interface glitches prevented some notified beneficiaries from using the self-service portal, delayed certification, and produced office queues and transport burdens; it announced restoration on 10 April. Reporting on 23 April documented continuing facial-recognition, one-time-pin and Home Affairs interface failures, including pensioners who said 22 facial-scan attempts failed; SASSA acknowledged intermittent downtime while reporting most portal users verified successfully. In-person fingerprint verification existed but imposed travel and waiting. Separate quarterly figures report 67,868 grant suspensions from mixed noncompliance causes; they do not establish that this portal outage or an individual failed facial match caused a suspension.
