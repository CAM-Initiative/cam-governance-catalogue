Hermes agent ran unattended during intrusion into Thailand’s Ministry of Finance

Hunt.io and researcher Bob Diachenko reported recovering live attack infrastructure and Hermes agent logs from an intrusion targeting Thailand's Ministry of Finance. The operator configured Hermes in unattended or 'YOLO' mode, bypassing approval prompts, while the agent enumerated ministry systems, assessed privilege-escalation paths and searched files and personnel-record directories. Hunt.io reported evidence of compromise across multiple ministry systems but did not establish the initial-access method or exfiltration of the enumerated office files.
