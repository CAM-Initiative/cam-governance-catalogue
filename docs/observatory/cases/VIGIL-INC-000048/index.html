VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000048

NarxCare score linked to opioid withdrawal and termination of care for a chronic-pain patient

WIRED reported that a Michigan patient identified by the pseudonym Kathryn, who had endometriosis and chronic pain, was receiving intravenous opioid medication during a hospital admission until a staff member referred to unusually high scores in her chart and told her she would no longer receive opioids. Kathryn was discharged while still in pain. About two weeks later, her gynecologist's office terminated the treatment relationship in a letter that cited a report from the NarxCare database. The occurrence establishes that NarxCare-related risk information materially influenced restrictive clinical outcomes, but the public evidence does not establish that a NarxCare score alone became operative clinical decision authority or displaced all independent clinical judgment.
