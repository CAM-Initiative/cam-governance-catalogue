VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000080

Google Maps propagation of U.S. 'Lake America' designation

After a U.S. executive order directed federal authorities to rename Lake Ontario as 'Lake America' and the U.S. Geographic Names Information System was updated, Google Maps adopted the U.S. designation for users in the United States. Google stated that Canadian users would continue to see 'Lake Ontario' and users outside the United States and Canada would see both names. Reuters subsequently reported that Ontario authorities were auditing government websites after the U.S.-derived designation appeared through services using Google Maps data.
