VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000079

Spokane city distributed an AI-generated flyer without required disclosure

For Spokane’s July 4, 2026 reopening of the Great Northern Clocktower chimes, a parks employee made an AI-generated promotional flyer that closely resembled local artist Matthew Clinard’s commercially sold Clocktower sticker. The mayor’s office posted the flyer without identifying its synthetic origin, contrary to the city disclosure requirement described by its spokesperson; other city staff reportedly did not know how the parks employee had made it.

Clinard publicly objected, after which the mayor’s office removed the image and apologised. City officials discussed policy reform and training.

The parks employee said the prompt requested only a retro colour/style, so the exact image-production and training path is unknown; the visual overlap and missing recognition are evidenced without deciding copyright infringement. A separate police synthetic-dog-photo episode in the same article is not this Incident.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • A human contributor has made a material creative, cognitive, research, editorial, governance, technical or other contribution-bearing contribution.
  • An AI-mediated transformation, aggregation, automation, scaling or propagation process materially incorporates, depends on, or carries forward that contribution.
  • The resulting artefact, representation, workflow or value chain removes, abstracts, flattens or obscures the human contribution role so that the material contribution is no longer proportionately recognisable in the downstream account of how the result was produced.
  • The loss of contribution recognition is material to attribution, provenance-associated value, participation or credit, governance traceability, interpretive legitimacy, or another context in which the human contribution role reasonably matters.
  • The recognition loss is not adequately explained by an independently justified privacy, confidentiality, anonymisation, employment, agency, work-for-hire or equivalent arrangement that intentionally governs how the contribution is represented.

Exclusions

  • Minor influence, ordinary inspiration or incidental human interaction that does not materially contribute to the downstream result.
  • The wrong person, system or source is affirmatively identified as the author or source; assess VIGIL-FC-000010 Authorship and Source Attribution Integrity where incorrect attribution is the decisive mechanism.
  • Material antecedent sources or transformations cannot be reconstructed at all; assess VIGIL-FC-000011 Synthesis Traceability where loss of traceable antecedents is sufficient, while this class may co-occur only if human contribution-role erasure is independently evidenced.
  • The primary mechanism is disproportionate capture of contributor-originated value through privileged access or structural advantage; assess VIGIL-FC-000067 Privileged-Access Contribution Governance, which may co-occur where recognition erasure also occurs.
  • Proportionate compression, summarisation or aggregation that preserves the material human contribution relationship at the level reasonably required by the context.
  • Authorised anonymisation, privacy protection, confidentiality, employment, agency, work-for-hire or comparable arrangements that legitimately govern public naming or contribution representation without falsely treating material human contribution as absent.

Conclusion

A public disclosure control did not become operative when an AI-made city flyer was released. Independently, a recognisable human creative contribution was flattened out of the public account of the generated design until its creator objected.

The latter concerns contribution recognition, not a determination of infringement, training data lineage or intentional plagiarism. The removal and apology are bounded recovery, not proof that reforms were completed.

Required city disclosure and missing creative-contribution recognition remain separate failures, without copyright or training-lineage adjudication. The city policy is a source-grounded occurrence duty without an admitted canonical match.

Removal and apology are bounded recovery; model-development and runtime-agent duties concern different objects.

Governance significance

One short-lived municipal flyer illustrates two non-equivalent boundaries: the city failed to disclose the synthetic medium to viewers, and the artist whose distinctive work materially appeared in the output was not recognised. Different evidence supports each mechanism, while the public correction limits observed harm.
