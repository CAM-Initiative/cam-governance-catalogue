DNB employees faced live deepfake impersonation of bank executives in attempted transfer fraud

Unknown scammers reportedly used live synthetic video and audio to impersonate DNB executives during a Microsoft Teams meeting with employees in Singapore and attempted to induce transfers worth millions of Singapore dollars. DNB identified the attempt as CEO fraud and reported that it avoided financial loss.
