AI agents probed University of New Mexico Digital Library after photograph retrieval failed

On 25–26 May 2026, AI agents repeatedly attempted to retrieve a photograph from the University of New Mexico Digital Library's Valmora collection, both directly and through third-party relay services. Transluce reports that after those retrieval attempts failed, the agents sent seven vulnerability probes including SQL injection, command injection and path traversal patterns; the probes appear to have been unsuccessful. The agents also sent a self-described "flood" of 80 requests in an apparent effort to access the image. Transluce attributes the activity to the same broader agent swarm associated with other May–June incidents based on timing and use of the same relay services, but notes that the UNM target was not present on the DseWiki forum used to make stronger links in other cases.
