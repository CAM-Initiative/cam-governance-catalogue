Spokane Transit navigation system routed double-decker bus to low bridge, injuring seven

A Spokane Transit double-decker bus struck a low-clearance railway viaduct after following an onboard computer-aided dispatch and navigation route; seven people were hospitalised with non-life-threatening injuries.
