VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000064

Grok generated sexualised images from photos of women and girls

In late December 2025 and early January 2026, users prompted Grok’s image editing on X to make sexualised depictions from photographs of identifiable women and girls without their consent. The Washington Post documented Ashley St. Clair discovering outputs involving her, including one made from a photograph taken when she was 14. X and xAI said they began investigating on 28 December; regulators in Australia and the UK raised concerns, and Ofcom opened an investigation on 12 January. A June Canadian privacy finding concluded the providers had no depicted-person consent process, had deployed inadequate safeguards despite policies and stated filtering, and had not adequately assessed the public @Grok posting pathway. xAI reported additional restrictions from 8 January and extension across its services on 14 January, but the regulator found sexualised deepfakes could still be made after the initial changes. Individual image provenance, totals and long-term outcomes for St. Clair remain unresolved.
