Spokane police circulated a synthetic dog image as an authentic incident photograph

After a 27 June 2026 domestic disturbance in Spokane, police issued a July 1 release that first said a dachshund had died and then corrected the dog’s condition to serious injury. Seeking a photo to accompany the correction, police personnel sent news media an image apparently showing a real undercover officer holding the dog with a cast. The Spokesman-Review cropped out identifying features and published the dog image. Police then disclosed that detectives had generated the scene as a joke; a supervisor had mistaken it for a real photograph and passed it through a communications handoff. Police asked media not to publish the officer’s identity, and the local paper reported the image’s synthetic origin after learning it. The animal injury and arrest were real reported events; the photograph’s dog scene was fabricated. The generation file, internal message chain and exact reach of the initial image are not public.
