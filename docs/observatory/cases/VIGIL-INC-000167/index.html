Zoetis Vetscan Imagyst allegedly misclassified canine cancer, followed by repeat surgery and death

Columbia Veterinary Hospital in The Dalles, Oregon sued Zoetis in July 2026 over its Vetscan Imagyst diagnostic service. The hospital alleges it bought the system in 2024 relying on expert-level diagnostic claims.

In October 2025, it used the software to analyse a rapidly growing neck mass in an eleven-year-old dog. The result allegedly identified cancer-associated cell features but categorised the growth as inflammatory.

Staff reportedly relied on that interpretation for surgery. Later Oregon State University pathology identified aggressive cancer and incomplete removal.

The dog reportedly required a second operation and died from complications. The complaint alleges that Zoetis changed the original report after notification and that representatives apologised and acknowledged similar errors during a call.

The hospital sought at least $50,000. A September docket listing records removal to federal court and pretrial scheduling.
