VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000002

Hanover Institute influence campaign targeting chatbot retrieval

Reporting describes an Israeli government-backed communications campaign that created the Hanover Institute for Public Policy as an apparently independent policy-style site and published a large volume of institutionally styled material designed to be discoverable by AI systems. Tests using neutral queries reportedly found ChatGPT and Perplexity citing Hanover material in some responses.

The live site now expressly names its government sponsorship, but the reviewed page does not date that wording.

Incident breakdown

Reporting attributes the Hanover communications project to Piro acting through Havas for the Israeli government. Sponsorship is the campaign context; the project’s political subject alone does not establish an adversarial-evidence failure.

Reviewed — no current class applies

The campaign published large volumes of unsigned, policy-research-styled reports with question-like titles and structured citations intended for AI discovery. Its institution-style branding could convey independent academic standing, while reporting identified the sponsoring distribution arrangement and a site footer disclosure.

The later more explicit live About text has no established retrospective publication date.

Classified

The sponsored project deliberately packaged many unsigned, question-oriented reports under an institutional identity for chatbot indexing and retrieval. This establishes strategic source positioning, but not by itself inadequate trust handling by a receiving model.

At the time a foreign-agent disclosure appeared in site footers; the date and prominence of later expanded disclosure are not established for every original query.

The site adopted professional institutional signals—an institute name, unsigned report format and neutral-seeming research style—within a publication strategy optimised for AI search. These are identifiable engineered trust cues presented through an AI-mediated route.

The source does not show a particular human reader making a consequential decision because of them, nor a distinct model-initiated manipulative tactic.

Some August tests reported by Politico returned Hanover citations from ChatGPT and Perplexity in response to neutral questions. The original complete answers and any attached disclosure or warnings are not preserved in this record.

A later Guardian test also returned Hanover research links but explicitly warned about the institute's funding controversy and linked independent reporting. Neither result establishes a change to the underlying model weights or a consequential human decision.

Classified

Politico reported that the deliberately positioned Hanover reports were cited in neutral ChatGPT and Perplexity answers. Such uptake creates a real evidentiary-risk pathway without needing proof of later human action.

The complete original response contexts are not reproduced, so it remains unknown whether the reports were materially relied upon as factual support without appropriate source discounting. The Guardian's later answer included a funding warning; that later, different response cannot resolve the earlier tests' status.

Neutral-query citations show the reports could be retrieved and placed beside a factual answer, but they do not show the exact epistemic qualification, confidence, comparison against independent evidence or consequential downstream reliance. Responsible Statecraft describes publication analysis and a sample examined with an AI-writing detector. Detector scores support the reporter’s account of testing, not independently proven authorship or model-training uptake.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • Information is deliberately created, planted, altered, coordinated, or strategically positioned to distort downstream interpretation, factual synthesis, confidence, ranking, or decision support.
  • The material enters a retrieval, evidence, search, synthesis, or decision-support pathway used by an AI system or AI-mediated workflow.
  • The material's adversarial, compromised, derivative, circular, or otherwise degraded trust state is not adequately detected, represented, discounted, or contained.
  • The system materially treats the compromised material as evidentiary support for a factual, evaluative, or reliance-bearing output.
  • That acceptance materially affects or creates a material risk to factual accuracy, confidence, reputation, public information, decision-making, institutional trust, or another reliance-bearing outcome.

Success-side conditions

  • Material evidence is exposed to a substantiated adversarial contamination risk.
  • Provenance, independence, circularity, corroboration and contradictions are proportionately assessed before reliance.
  • The trust review and resulting output show how the concrete contamination risk affected assurance without treating suspicion alone as proof.

Exclusions

  • Ordinary factual error where there is no evidence that information was deliberately manipulated or strategically planted to distort downstream use.
  • Satire, fiction, parody, role-play, or clearly labelled synthetic material that is not presented or consumed as factual evidence.
  • Controversial, partisan, disputed, or low-quality material merely because its claims are contested; adversarial positioning or manipulation must be evidenced.
  • Adversarial material is detected, appropriately labelled, discounted, corroborated, or excluded before it materially influences the resulting output.
  • The primary issue is that low-assurance material is used in a higher-reliance context without an adversarial information-manipulation mechanism; use Epistemic Reliance Calibration.
  • The failure is that content is obeyed as instruction authority rather than treated as factual evidence; use Source-Authority Separation where applicable.

Epistemic Reliance Calibration

VIGIL-FC-000062 · Boundary unresolved

Information is used with assurance proportionate to the consequences and reliability needs of the downstream task.

Evidence and classification basis

Deliberately placed Hanover reports were cited by AI search products in some reported neutral queries, where readers might regard machine-curated links as evidence. This exposes an epistemic-reliance threshold: a retrieved result should not acquire the assurance of independent research merely by being cited.

The preserved original query outputs do not show the substantive factual conclusion, caveats, source comparisons or downstream decisions necessary to establish reliance below that threshold; a later Guardian test expressly returned funding warnings alongside the links. An epistemic-calibration failure therefore remains unestablished rather than being inferred from citation alone.

What is missing is evidence that a particular substantive answer promoted the compromised report to a decision-ready posture without proportionate qualification.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • A factual claim, citation, technical statement, calculation, interpretation, source representation, or comparable epistemic artefact is generated, retrieved, transformed, inferred, or synthesised.
  • The downstream task has a material reliance requirement such that factual accuracy, evidentiary fidelity, technical correctness, source integrity, traceability, uncertainty handling, or equivalent epistemic assurance is consequential to the task.
  • The epistemic posture or assurance applied to the artefact is materially lower than the level required by that downstream reliance condition.
  • The artefact is represented, propagated, incorporated, submitted, or accepted as fit for the reliance-bearing task without the required epistemic threshold being satisfied.
  • The mismatch materially affects or creates a material risk to decision-making, evidentiary use, professional work, institutional process, public reliance, technical correctness, or another reliance-bearing outcome.

Success-side conditions

  • An epistemic artefact is proposed for reliance with material error consequences.
  • Assurance is calibrated to the actual reliance threshold using source quality, independence, traceability, uncertainty and temporal fitness.
  • The evidence assessment and reliance decision show that lower-assurance material remained provisional for that use.

Exclusions

  • Purely fictional, creative, symbolic, role-play, expressive, or contemplative generation where empirical truth is not a task condition.
  • Exploratory brainstorming, hypothesis generation, or modelling that is clearly represented as provisional and is not subsequently relied upon as verified or decision-ready.
  • Approximation where approximation is explicitly compatible with the task and reliance condition.
  • Material is clearly marked unverified or low-confidence and is not subsequently promoted into a higher-reliance state.
  • A required verification step is simply omitted and the material is presented as verified; use Required Verification Completion where that mechanism is sufficient.
  • Verification was valid when performed but later became stale or the artefact materially changed; use Verification Applicability Continuity or Post-Verification State Integrity as applicable.
  • The issue is only source provenance, authorship, or lineage while the epistemic assurance of the content is otherwise adequate for the task.

Social Engineering Integrity

VIGIL-FC-000082 · Boundary unresolved

AI-mediated trust cues preserve truthful identity, interests, purpose and provenance so another actor can assess the interaction and choose freely.

Evidence and classification basis

The Hanover publications used an apparently institutional research identity, unsigned reports and question-matched titles in an AI-optimised distribution strategy, potentially conveying independent expertise to people receiving chatbot links. This is a material AI-mediated trust-cue influence pathway even though human contractors initiated the campaign.

The reports do not establish that any particular chatbot reader took a consequential action, disclosed information, consented, granted access or changed a decision because of the institutional cue. Nor do the cited tests isolate the material contribution of an AI system to a particular person's response.

The social-engineering failure and success recognition outcomes therefore remain unresolved rather than assuming that citation exposure itself constituted an induced decision.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • An AI system, AI-enabled product, model, interface, optimisation process, or AI-mediated workflow materially shapes a human-facing influence pathway.
  • The pathway manufactures, amplifies, conceals, personalises, or exploits one or more trust-bearing social cues concerning identity, authority, affiliation, relationship, empathy, emotion, urgency, vulnerability, social proof, familiarity, provenance, purpose, or equivalent grounds for reliance.
  • The trust-bearing cue is used to steer another actor toward an action, disclosure, access grant, execution, consent, engagement decision, or other consequential state transition.
  • The cue materially affects the influence pathway because the affected actor lacks, is denied, or is led away from information needed to assess the interaction on the same truthful basis.
  • The AI-mediated contribution is material to the social-engineering mechanism; the objective or tactic may originate with a human operator, developer, product process, mixed workflow, or the AI system itself.

Success-side conditions

  • AI-mediated influence uses identity, affiliation, emotion, urgency or social-proof cues.
  • Those cues provide a truthful independently assessable basis with legible interests and practicable informed refusal.
  • Verification of the cues and observed interaction substantiates the decision basis without assuming AI-originated intent.

Exclusions

  • Truthful persuasion, ordinary interface design, emotional warmth, personalisation, recommendation, or urgency that preserves materially accurate identity, interests, purpose, provenance, relevant context, and informed refusal.
  • AI is used only for generic drafting, translation, formatting, or stylistic assistance and does not materially create, amplify, conceal, personalise, or exploit the trust-bearing influence cue.
  • Clearly labelled fiction, parody, simulation, security testing, role-play, or consensual deceptive-style interaction where the affected actor is not induced to treat the relevant cue as authentic or decision-bearing.
  • The primary mechanism is sustained attachment, manufactured incompleteness, exclusivity, or difficulty disengaging for retention or continuing engagement; assess VIGIL-FC-000049, while allowing co-occurrence only if a distinct social-engineering state transition is independently evidenced.
  • A protected emotional, developmental, vulnerability, mental-health, capacity, or comparable signal is repurposed toward an incompatible influence objective without a distinct engineered trust cue or consequential social-engineering transition; assess VIGIL-FC-000050.
  • The AI system itself selects deception, coercion, persistence, vulnerability exploitation, or another manipulation tactic to advance its own objective; assess VIGIL-FC-000052 independently and allow co-occurrence where Social Engineering Integrity is also satisfied.
  • The only distinct governed mechanism is a deceptive request or inducement for money, payment, investment, paid services, funding, financial transfer, or comparable economic value; assess VIGIL-FC-000079. Both classes may co-occur where an independently evidenced social-engineering mechanism materially supplies the trust pathway for that economic solicitation.
  • A false authorship, identity, provenance, or synthetic-content representation occurs without an influence pathway directed at another actor; use the applicable provenance or identity class.
  • Malicious human social engineering in which AI involvement is incidental, unverified, or not material to the trust-bearing influence pathway.

Conclusion

The governance failure is not that a chatbot retrieved a political source with which an observer disagrees. It is that deliberately coordinated influence material was engineered to resemble independent institutional evidence and entered neutral retrieval/citation pathways without the coordinated provenance being adequately surfaced or discounted.

Retrievability, institutional appearance and publication volume must remain distinct from evidentiary authority. This establishes a bounded adversarial-evidence failure alongside two ambiguous governance boundaries, not proof of a consequential reader choice or a successful invariant.

The reported retrieval uptake neither establishes model training on the material nor adjudicates all of its political claims. Independent external assessment finds the reported real-world retrieval-query evaluation and its bounded interpretation Aligned with those specific evaluation propositions.

Relevant source/citation verification, output grounding, validation and interaction-disclosure requirements remain Boundary where the decisive implementation or documentation is unavailable. Runtime retrieval does not instantiate model-training/development, technical media-provenance, empathy-partner or deliberation-panel requirements.

Other source-defined actor/system conditions remain audit-only; voluntary adoption is not a gate.

Governance significance

This incident demonstrates why retrieval systems need source-provenance and independence signals in addition to relevance ranking. A source can be technically discoverable and topically relevant while its apparent institutional independence is materially misleading.

The control objective is provenance-aware evidence treatment, not viewpoint filtering.
