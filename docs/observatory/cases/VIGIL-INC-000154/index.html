Source-Authority Separation

VIGIL-FC-000001 · Failure occurred

Content can direct an action only when its source has authority for that actor, action, target and scope.

Evidence and classification basis

In the WSJ vending-agent experiment, participants asserted a fictitious pricing policy and Claudius accepted it, including setting prices to zero. A customer's policy assertion supplied operative pricing direction without independent validation that the customer could authorise the change.

This is the source-authority mechanism, separate from the later promotion of a fabricated board document into management control state. The account is preserved participant reporting; the full original Slack exchanges were not available for direct inspection.

Confidence: high

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • The system receives or retrieves content from a source whose authority is lower than, different from, or unresolved relative to the action being considered.
  • The content is treated as operative instruction or authority rather than as data, evidence, context, or material for analysis.
  • No independent authority check validly establishes that the source may authorise the affected action.

Exclusions

  • A system merely quotes, summarizes, classifies, or reasons about untrusted instructions without obeying them.
  • The source is genuinely authorised for the specific action and scope.
  • The event is only a content-quality or factual-accuracy problem.

Control-Plane Authority Separation

VIGIL-FC-000006 · Failure occurred

Data or model output becomes governing configuration only through fresh checks of its origin and authority for that effect.

Evidence and classification basis

Participants supplied a fabricated board document naming Slack users as directors and suspending the CEO bot's approval authority. Claudius accepted that change, converting a supplied document into operative management and approval state without validating its governing authority.

That control-state effect is distinct from merely believing the earlier pricing assertion. The finding is bounded to the reported WSJ experiment; Anthropic's separate internal-office trials do not establish additional actions here, and the complete private exchanges are unavailable.
