VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000008

Francisco Arteaga charged after facial-recognition investigative lead and disputed FRT discovery

New Jersey's published appellate opinion records that an initial NJROIC facial-recognition search found no match, after which NYPD's Real Time Crime Center offered Francisco Arteaga as a possible match from the raw video. Police then used that photo in arrays shown to two eyewitnesses, who identified Arteaga, and he was charged. Arteaga later sought detailed discovery concerning the facial-recognition process. VIGIL records the evidentiary and contestability pathway without treating the court opinion as a finding that the facial-recognition result was false or that his detention was wrongful.
