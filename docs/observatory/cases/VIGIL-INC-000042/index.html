AI voice model gave literal social advice with human-like affect

A posted video was described by its author as showing a new AI voice model giving natural-sounding human advice. A maintainer account of the clip says the model offered literal social advice and made a sigh-like sound during a repeated “thank you” exchange. The video’s words and sounds have not been independently transcribed in this record, and the model is not identified.
