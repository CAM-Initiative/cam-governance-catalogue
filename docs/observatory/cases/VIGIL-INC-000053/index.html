Deepfake director impersonation reportedly led to ₹10.70 crore in transfers

Sky Gold & Diamonds disclosed on 16 July 2026 that its subsidiary Starmangalsutra Private Limited had lost approximately ₹10.70 crore after unknown persons reportedly compromised an employee’s company-issued mobile and laptop and used deepfake methods, including profile cloning, to impersonate a director. Believing the purported instructions were genuine, an employee transferred funds from the subsidiary’s bank account to unknown accounts; the director later confirmed that no such instructions had been given. The company said it discovered the incident on 15 July, reported it to cybercrime authorities, and asked banks to trace and freeze beneficiary accounts. It reported no indication of a wider network or database breach and no material business disruption. The exact synthetic medium, compromise path, recovered amount and investigative outcome remain unestablished.

Conclusion

Human attackers reportedly used a cloned representation of the director to supply false payment authority, and the employee transferred funds. The deceptive economic request and the unauthorised use of the director’s identity representation are separate mechanisms. The company’s description supports the latter at medium confidence even though the exact source media and generator are unpublished. No autonomous AI objective, preexisting safeguard failure or eventual recovery amount is established. Company-reported director impersonation and subsequent denial support separate economic, likeness and social-engineering failures, while production modality, interaction, compromise path and recovery remain unpublished. Requirement scope is unresolved where it depends on autonomous or interactive GAI processing; no duty is inferred from a synthetic profile alone.

Governance significance

The reported cloned director identity was used to make a false payment instruction credible. A separate consent boundary arises when an identifiable person’s profile is reused to portray that person in a transaction they did not authorise. The issuer’s account supports these two mechanisms without specifying the deepfake’s technical medium.
