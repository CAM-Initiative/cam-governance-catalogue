VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000169

Border Patrol facial recognition identification reportedly preceded Global Entry revocation

Nicole Cleland says an agent at a Minneapolis ICE operation addressed her by name on 10 January and said agents had facial recognition and body-camera recording. She received notice three days later that Global Entry and TSA PreCheck privileges were revoked.

She reported fear and paused observing ICE activity while continuing peaceful protests. The notice did not give a definite reason.
