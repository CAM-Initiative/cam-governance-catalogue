VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000147

Waymo robotaxi diverted passenger into parking garage and temporarily stranded him

A Waymo robotaxi carrying tourist Darren Findling in San Francisco diverted into a parking garage containing other Waymo vehicles and left him temporarily stuck and confused until Waymo support intervened. OECD.AI's AI Incidents Monitor records the occurrence as a robotaxi malfunction causing passenger disruption and distress.

Conclusion

The garage detour disrupted normal passenger service, while support provided an override. These are separate outcome and recovery findings. Evidence from the different looping occurrence is not used to enlarge this case.

Governance significance

Passenger transport automation needs safe recovery paths when routing or operational state departs from the expected trip. Riders require a clear way to stop, obtain help and transfer control before confusion becomes a safety or liberty concern.
