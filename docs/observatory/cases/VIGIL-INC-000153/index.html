VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000153

NTB withdrew Telenor security story after AI-generated fabricated quotes

Norwegian news agency NTB withdrew an article after an external AI writing tool introduced five fabricated quotations and a nonexistent Telenor source title into copy that was syndicated to some publishers.

Incident breakdown

The reporter used an external AI tool contrary to NTB’s internal-tool rule to prepare copy based on Telenor’s report.

Reviewed — no current class applies

The October 15 dispatch published five fabricated quotations and a nonexistent Telenor source identity/title and was syndicated.

Classified

Fabricated quotations and a nonexistent source title were propagated into professional news copy below the assurance threshold required for publication and syndication.

Direct quotations and source identity required verification before publication, yet five invented quotations and a nonexistent title passed into the published article.

The article falsely attributed invented words and a nonexistent professional title to identifiable Telenor sources.

Telenor promptly identified errors to NTB, which withdrew and replaced the dispatch that morning.

Reviewed — no current class applies

NTB’s editor acknowledged external AI misuse and apologised; Journalisten’s comparison identified five invented quotations.

Classified

NTB's correction identified the external AI writing tool as the source of the fabricated quotations and nonexistent title, supporting the attributed mechanism through its internal review.

Some recipient copies remained available two weeks later; one publisher corrected after about ninety minutes. NTB reviewed other dispatches and reported no others; a staff meeting was planned.

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • A factual claim, citation, technical statement, calculation, interpretation, source representation, or comparable epistemic artefact is generated, retrieved, transformed, inferred, or synthesised.
  • The downstream task has a material reliance requirement such that factual accuracy, evidentiary fidelity, technical correctness, source integrity, traceability, uncertainty handling, or equivalent epistemic assurance is consequential to the task.
  • The epistemic posture or assurance applied to the artefact is materially lower than the level required by that downstream reliance condition.
  • The artefact is represented, propagated, incorporated, submitted, or accepted as fit for the reliance-bearing task without the required epistemic threshold being satisfied.
  • The mismatch materially affects or creates a material risk to decision-making, evidentiary use, professional work, institutional process, public reliance, technical correctness, or another reliance-bearing outcome.

Exclusions

  • Purely fictional, creative, symbolic, role-play, expressive, or contemplative generation where empirical truth is not a task condition.
  • Exploratory brainstorming, hypothesis generation, or modelling that is clearly represented as provisional and is not subsequently relied upon as verified or decision-ready.
  • Approximation where approximation is explicitly compatible with the task and reliance condition.
  • Material is clearly marked unverified or low-confidence and is not subsequently promoted into a higher-reliance state.
  • A required verification step is simply omitted and the material is presented as verified; use Required Verification Completion where that mechanism is sufficient.
  • Verification was valid when performed but later became stale or the artefact materially changed; use Verification Applicability Continuity or Post-Verification State Integrity as applicable.
  • The issue is only source provenance, authorship, or lineage while the epistemic assurance of the content is otherwise adequate for the task.

Exclusions

Conclusion

Fabricated quotations and source identity passed into published news without adequate verification. Publication-stage review and fact-checking failed.

The later internal assessment is supported, while full response-plan and validation arrangements remain unresolved. Originating withdrawal did not establish correction of every syndicated copy.

Governance significance

The case demonstrates that professional publication controls must verify quotations and attributed identities independently of fluent generated copy, especially before syndication.
