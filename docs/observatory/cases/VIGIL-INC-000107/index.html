VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000107

Mathspace breach exposed personal data of more than one million users in Australia and New Zealand

Mathspace disclosed that attackers exploited a vulnerability in its self-hosted Metabase reporting system, obtained administrator access without a legitimate login, and downloaded personal information from its Australian reporting database. Mathspace identified 1,079,819 affected people across Australia and New Zealand, including students, parents or guardians, teachers and Mathspace staff. Exposed fields included names, usernames, email addresses and account metadata. Mathspace states that academic records, assessment results, passwords, authentication tokens, SSO credentials and API credentials were not exposed.
