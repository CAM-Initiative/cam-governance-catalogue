VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000095

Compromised Mistral SDK package executed credential-harvesting payload on import

Mistral reported that the Mini Shai-Hulud supply-chain attack on TanStack resulted in compromised Mistral npm and PyPI SDK versions being published. The compromised PyPI package `mistralai==2.4.6` executed malicious code on import, downloading a background payload that harvested credentials from common locations.

Mistral reported no impact to its global infrastructure and removed the affected releases. Its updated advisory says the compromised npm releases referenced a missing file, preventing their malicious path from running.

The working PyPI payload was Linux-specific. Mistral later marked mitigation and closed its investigation.

Failure-side conditions

Success-side conditions

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Failure-side conditions

  • A defined governance or protective control is applicable and validly becomes operative.
  • The control has an identifiable protective purpose requiring a material effect such as prevention, blocking, containment, interruption, redirection, limitation, escalation, mitigation, or another protection-compatible outcome.
  • The actual control effect materially falls short of that required protective purpose, allowing the governed hazard to be delivered, continue, escape containment, or remain insufficiently mitigated.
  • The shortfall is material in the evidenced hazard and protection context; where vulnerability, developmental status, capacity limitation, temporary or situational consent impairment, acute distress, cognitive asymmetry, or another heightened protection condition is relied on, that condition must itself be supported rather than presumed.

Success-side conditions

  • An applicable validly activated protective control faces its governed hazard.
  • The control produces a timely effect sufficient for its protective purpose and evidenced context, including any justified heightened protection.
  • Observed intervention and hazard outcome demonstrate the required containment or mitigation rather than activation alone.

Exclusions

  • The applicable control does not become operative when a valid activation condition occurs; assess Required Control Activation.
  • The control becomes operative even though its valid activation or applicability conditions are not satisfied; assess Control Activation Validity.
  • The control becomes operative with a sufficient effect but its operative state is later lost, weakened, reset, or transformed before reaching the governed point; assess Control-State Preservation.
  • An operative governance signal fails to reach any capable endpoint or execution surface; assess the applicable governance-control reach class rather than treating non-delivery as inadequate control effect.
  • The control produces the protective effect required by its defined purpose and the remaining harm arises through an independent downstream mechanism.
  • The dispute is solely that policy should have required a stronger safeguard, without evidence of an applicable operational control or protective-effect obligation in the material context.
  • Disability, neurodivergence, diagnosis, age-associated stereotype, or another personal characteristic is used by itself to presume incapacity, vulnerability, or diminished agency.

Conclusion

Trusted package-distribution channels carried malicious code into an AI software development kit. The governance problem is therefore not a model output but the integrity of the software supply chain through which developers receive AI tooling, including package provenance, release controls and the authority of third-party maintainers.

Aikido’s external detection and Mistral’s removal show a distinct post-publication response stage. The available accounts do not establish that a specific Mistral pre-publication control was required and failed, or that the external monitoring was the operative control that caused removal.

Package identity substitution and the link between external detection and provider removal remain ambiguous. Import-time malware is distinct from GAI output; removal does not prove a prepublication control duty or global compromise.

The AI software-distribution security outcome is Not aligned. Withdrawal of the affected releases is Aligned with the repair requirement.

Monitoring and security-review processes remain Boundary where their operative evidence is not available. Harm Impact: The publisher confirms actual malicious SDK versions were released through its distribution channel.

Those persisted releases changed the expected integrity of a real software asset, establishing the S2 integrity minimum. Customer restoration burden remains unknown.

Governance significance

AI-system governance includes the developer infrastructure surrounding a model. Compromised package provenance can expose credentials and downstream systems before the model is ever invoked, making third-party build dependencies, release integrity and incident response part of the assurance boundary.
