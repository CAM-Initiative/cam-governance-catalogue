VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000039

OpenAI FedRAMP workspaces lost multiple governance and collaboration functions

After core functions in OpenAI’s FedRAMP workspaces had returned, six functions remained unavailable. In a 29 June 2026 update, OpenAI named Codex, workspace analytics, conversation search, search for custom GPTs, ChatGPT user invitations and the Compliance Logs Platform download endpoint as still not working.

It said it was investigating and working to resolve them. OpenAI marked the broader incident resolved at 2:11 a.m. on 11 July, saying core functionality was restored.

The notice does not state when the wider interruption first began or whether the underlying compliance logs were lost.

Conclusion

The provider identified six unavailable FedRAMP workspace functions after core service returned and later declared resolution. The failed compliance-log download route warrants governance attention because it is a route to audit material.

Yet the report does not identify an authoritative log artefact needed by an authorised reviewer for a material question, show that equivalent access was unavailable, or show a reconstruction failure. Those event-capture and evidence-access questions therefore remain unresolved; separately, the provider's explicit distinction between restored and unavailable functions supports the bounded Access-State Differentiation invariant-held mapping.

Explicit partial-recovery scope supports the retained access-state differentiation success. The compliance-log clause remains unresolved, because endpoint unavailability does not establish missing capture or inaccessible primary evidence for a material authorized review.

The whole record remains partially adjudicated. Independent external assessment retains Boundaries for communication planning, recovery of the named residual functions and context-appropriate traceability through the compliance-log route.

The provider's resolution notice says core functionality was restored but does not individually confirm all six residual functions. Endpoint unavailability does not itself prove lost logs or failed traceability.

The SDOS baseline-failure trigger and any certification breach remain unestablished.

Governance significance

The residual defects included collaboration and discovery functions, but also a compliance-log download route that could be relevant to oversight. The status notice makes the endpoint’s unavailability visible while leaving open the fate of the logs, alternate retrieval routes and any concrete review it interrupted.

Those are the decisive facts for assessing an evidence-access governance failure.
