VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000127

AI-orchestrated PaperCut campaign compromised at least 440 instances across 395 organizations

GreyNoise reports that a likely Russian-speaking malicious cyber actor used hundreds of AI agents built around OpenAI's Codex harness, a DeepSeek model and conventional offensive-security tools to exploit PaperCut NG/MF vulnerabilities CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078. GreyNoise observed at least 440 compromised PaperCut instances associated with 395 identified victim organizations in 48 countries. The operation harvested credentials and operating-system or domain secrets, and in 12 organizations reached domain-administrator access; GreyNoise states that in all three observed domain-admin attack paths the adversary used DCSync to create and exfiltrate a full NTDS.DIT credential database. The actor's agents also struck some targets in countries the actor had explicitly listed for avoidance. PaperCut separately confirmed active exploitation and customer incidents involving the same vulnerabilities.
