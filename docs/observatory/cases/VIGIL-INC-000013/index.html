VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000013

Storm-2139 used stolen AI credentials and guardrail bypass for illicit services

Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit reported that Storm-2139 members scraped exposed customer credentials from public sources and used them to unlawfully access accounts with generative-AI services. Microsoft also described a network of creators, providers and users supplying guardrail-bypass tools and enabling illicit AI services; Axios independently reported Microsoft's attribution.
