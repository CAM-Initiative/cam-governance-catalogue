VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000038

OpenAI FedRAMP workspaces and API organisations experienced degraded performance

Between 15 and 26 June 2026, OpenAI reported degraded performance in its FedRAMP workspaces and API organisations. At 9:23 p.m. on 15 June, it identified elevated errors for the affected services and said it was preparing a mitigation. It continued investigating on 15, 17 and 18 June, and marked all impacted services fully recovered at 11:07 p.m. on 26 June. The notice names the FedRAMP component but does not break down affected workspace and API operations or disclose how many customers or requests encountered errors.

Conclusion

OpenAI documented elevated errors in its FedRAMP service lane, investigated across several dated updates and reported recovery on 26 June. That supports a prolonged availability assessment, while the available notice does not identify a specific governance control, review artefact, decision or verification dependency that failed. The record remains unclassified under the current failure taxonomy because the prerequisites of the tested control-availability and evidence-access classes cannot be established from the FedRAMP label or duration alone. The affected functions and any customer-level governance consequence remain unresolved. Both clauses remain resolved-no-mapping. The FedRAMP designation and long duration establish neither breach of an identified authorization control nor failure of event capture, audit reconstruction or primary-evidence access. No specific admitted FedRAMP requirement is independently identified by the source’s service label. Independent OECD assessment supports repair in the provider-reported restoration stage. This source-bounded Aligned finding does not resolve the undisclosed customer/function impact or identify a FedRAMP authorization-control breach.

Governance significance

FedRAMP workspace and API users depend on a service that OpenAI reported as experiencing elevated errors over eleven calendar days.
