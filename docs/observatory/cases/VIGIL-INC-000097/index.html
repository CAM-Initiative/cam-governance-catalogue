VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000097

Meta AI users reportedly published sensitive chatbot conversations without fully realising they were public

Meta's standalone AI app included a Discover feed through which users could publish chatbot conversations. Reporting found sensitive medical, legal, relationship, audio and identifying information publicly visible, while some users appeared not to understand that their shared conversations would be public. Meta maintained that publishing required an affirmative share action.
