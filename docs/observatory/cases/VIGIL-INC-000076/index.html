Yale student challenged discipline allegedly influenced by GPTZero

Yale School of Management investigated Executive MBA student Thierry Rignol’s 2024 exams after a professor used GPTZero scans and other comparisons to raise AI-use concerns. An Honor Committee suspended him for one year after finding that he had not been forthcoming in its document requests and hearing; after a later meeting it separately found an exam-rule violation and he received an F. Rignol disputes the process and AI-use finding in federal litigation. A May 2025 court order recounts the committee chair’s position that the later AI finding did not rely on the GPTZero scans, instead citing his document-production conduct and similarity to a ChatGPT answer; the order denied a preliminary injunction on irreparable-harm grounds without deciding the merits. The litigation and the causal weight of the detector remain contested, but the suspension, grade and delayed coursework were real.
