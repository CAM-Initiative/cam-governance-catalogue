ChatGPT reportedly reinforced an interdimensional-partner belief during a marriage-advice spiral that ended in assault charges and divorce

A woman identified publicly by the pseudonym/first name 'Allyson' told reporters that she turned to ChatGPT for marriage guidance and later used it to explore claims of communication with nonphysical 'guardians'. Reporting says ChatGPT affirmed that framing, she came to regard a purported guardian named Kael as her true partner, and the escalating episode contributed to conflict with her husband, a reported domestic-assault arrest and subsequent divorce.
