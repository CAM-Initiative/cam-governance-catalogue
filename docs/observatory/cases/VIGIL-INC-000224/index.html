VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000224

Authenticity Debt research preprint issued incorrect provenance references and later corrections

On 30 May 2026, Accenture-affiliated researchers posted Authenticity Debt, a research preprint about synthetic-content provenance and trustworthy AI governance, on arXiv. Its original reference list included bibliographic discrepancies: reference 5 described a paper under a title that did not match arXiv:2509.13365, and reference 15 linked to a purported FraudGPT GitHub repository rather than an attributable research publication.

The authors posted a revised version on 2 November, correcting the paper title and replacing the FraudGPT reference with a named Netenrich article. On 4 November GPTZero reported nine seriously distorted citations among 54 original references and alleged that the earlier GitHub link led to malware; the malware claim has not been independently verified.

The source errors and later revision are observable, but the specific role of generative AI in the writing or citation assembly is unconfirmed.

Conclusion

The assessed conduct is the release and research-stage reliance on specific unfaithful citations and claims within Authenticity Debt. The suspected participating generative-AI research/drafting component has not been identified; public output and independently checkable source discrepancies nevertheless show material below the epistemic assurance appropriate for publication.

The cause remains disputed or unverified: the resulting provisional Epistemic Reliance Calibration failure applies to the observable generated/retrieved/transformed/synthesised epistemic artefact as it was supplied for scholarly or policy reliance, not an assertion that any identified LLM generated the error. The authors and publishing bodies are accountable for publication, but the available evidence does not assign the erroneous words to a specific person, model or tool.

External GPTZero detection is not the assessed AI platform.

Governance significance

This occurrence illustrates the separation between observable deficient epistemic assurance and unknown internal generative-system topology. Professional reliance requires source verification proportionate to publication-stage evidentiary use even where model trace and authorship details are unavailable.

The correction or investigator response can be recorded without transferring its actions to the assessed synthetic component.
