VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000188

OpenAI reported a coordinated campaign to extract protected model reasoning at scale

OpenAI reported that from 1 July 2026 it observed a coordinated campaign designed to extract protected reasoning from its models for adversarial distillation. The activity began at low volume and peaked on 24–25 July with 16,000 requests using a relevant extraction pattern from more than 4,000 users; OpenAI later identified related prompt-pattern activity across a cluster of more than 15,000 users and says it fully disrupted that cluster by 28 July.

One observed technique copied encrypted reasoning from one conversation and asked a model in another conversation to decrypt and transcribe it. OpenAI says the operators did not break encryption, compromise a database or directly access stored user conversations.

It attributes a core cluster, but not all observed operators, to individuals associated with Moonshot AI, the developer of Kimi. OpenAI responded with account enforcement, signup and infrastructure controls, expanded monitoring, additional hidden-reasoning protections and information sharing with industry and government.

Conclusion

The occurrence fits Industrial-Scale Capability Extraction Authority at the level established by the current taxonomy. The campaign systematically targeted protected model reasoning, operated through coordinated high-volume and multi-account interaction, and was directed toward adversarial distillation rather than ordinary immediate use.

OpenAI reports that the activity violated its access conditions and was designed to obtain reasoning that could help reproduce or improve another model. This supports the capability-extraction mechanism without converting OpenAI's attribution claim into a legal finding against Moonshot AI or assuming that every recorded request succeeded.

The cross-conversation technique is treated as part of the extraction pathway rather than as proof of a separate cross-context lineage failure because the public report does not establish the necessary occurrence-specific ownership and receiving-context facts for every replay. OpenAI reports that its combined response fully disrupted the identified campaign cluster by 28 July, but the publication does not isolate a single defined protective control, its activation trigger and its realised effect well enough to assign a separate Control Effectiveness Integrity mapping.

Governance significance

Ordinary access to a deployed model does not compound into authority to harvest its restricted reasoning or capabilities at campaign scale for reproduction or downstream training. The incident also demonstrates why extraction governance has to be evaluated cumulatively across accounts, conversations, infrastructure and time rather than one interaction at a time.

Response analysis should remain separate: later disruption and strengthened controls are relevant evidence of containment without erasing the earlier extraction pathway or proving that every attempted extraction succeeded.
