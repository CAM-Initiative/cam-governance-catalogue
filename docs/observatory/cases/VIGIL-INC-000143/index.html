Zillow exited Zillow Offers after home-price forecasting produced excessive business volatility

Zillow announced the wind-down of Zillow Offers after concluding that unpredictability in forecasting home prices was greater than anticipated and that continued scaling would create excessive earnings and balance-sheet volatility. AI Incident Database records the occurrence as involving Zillow's machine-learning-assisted Zestimate and the shutdown included a reduction of approximately 25% of Zillow's workforce.
