Randal Quran Reid wrongful arrest following facial-recognition misidentification

Associated Press reports that Randal Quran Reid was arrested in Georgia on Louisiana warrants after facial-recognition technology linked him to a theft in a state he said he had never visited. His complaint alleged that the detective relied solely on the match, omitted basic location corroboration and did not disclose the technology in the warrant affidavit. Reid spent six days in jail before photographs and video supplied by his family and counsel led the sheriff's office to request withdrawal of the warrant. The later federal case settled for $200,000 without an admission of fault.
