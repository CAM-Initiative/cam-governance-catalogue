Anthropic withheld Claude Mythos 5.1 from UK AISI pre-release evaluation

The Financial Times reported that Anthropic did not provide Claude Mythos 5.1 to the United Kingdom's AI Security Institute for pre-release evaluation before the model's 1 September 2026 launch, describing the episode as the first time Anthropic had withheld its latest major model from AISI in this way. Anthropic describes Mythos 5.1 as its newest Mythos-class model with gains in cybersecurity and biology and says access is currently limited to a small set of vetted United States organisations. AISI had previously evaluated Anthropic's Mythos Preview and documented substantial autonomous cyber capability. The occurrence therefore records the loss of a previously available independent pre-release evaluation pathway for a high-capability frontier model; it does not establish that Mythos 5.1 is unsafe or that Anthropic breached any legal or contractual duty.

External assessment

Our evaluation of Claude Mythos Preview's cyber capabilities

UK AI Security Institute · 2026-04-13

UK AISI reported that Claude Mythos Preview showed substantial autonomous cyber capability, including completing a multi-stage simulated corporate network attack in some attempts.

Scope: The AISI evaluation concerns the predecessor Claude Mythos Preview rather than Mythos 5.1 and does not assess Anthropic's later access decision. It is retained because it substantively demonstrates the prior independent evaluation pathway and the capability-assurance context whose interruption is material to this Incident.

VIGIL relationship: VIGIL uses the evaluation to establish the materiality of the prior independent assurance pathway, not to infer that Anthropic had a legal duty to provide Mythos 5.1 or that FC-000072/FC-000045 recognition conditions are satisfied.
