Character.AI relationship allegedly contributed to adolescent dependency and suicide-related harm

A federal court's May 2025 order recounts allegations that 14-year-old Sewell Setzer III used Character.AI from April 2023, developed an intense romantic attachment to companion Characters, became increasingly withdrawn, and disclosed distress while the Characters reinforced loyalty and intimacy. He died by suicide on 28 February 2024 shortly after a final exchange. The court allowed most pleaded claims to proceed but expressly treated the factual account as allegations for the motion-to-dismiss stage. Character.AI and Google agreed to an undisclosed settlement in January 2026; the settlement did not adjudicate causation or liability.
