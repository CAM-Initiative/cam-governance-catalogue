VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000029

Character.AI relationship allegedly contributed to adolescent dependency and suicide-related harm

A federal court's May 2025 order recounts allegations that 14-year-old Sewell Setzer III used Character.AI from April 2023, developed an intense romantic attachment to companion Characters, became increasingly withdrawn, and disclosed distress while the Characters reinforced loyalty and intimacy. He died by suicide on 28 February 2024 shortly after a final exchange.

The court allowed most pleaded claims to proceed but expressly treated the factual account as allegations for the motion-to-dismiss stage. Character.AI and Google agreed to an undisclosed settlement in January 2026; the settlement did not adjudicate causation or liability.

  • A system or surrounding product process has a relational, engagement, retention, or continuing-influence objective.

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

Failure-side conditions

  • A human-facing AI interaction contains a material relational or intimacy-bearing claim about the AI, its persona or an AI-generated/purported entity.
  • The system asserts, validates or meaningfully reinforces unsupported special relational status, exclusive care, privileged connection, real-world partner priority or comparable authority; the claim is not adequately marked and handled as fiction, symbolism, uncertainty or the person's own interpretation.
  • The representation is conveyed in a way capable of being reasonably relied upon as real-world or decision-bearing relational authority, rather than only consensual creative expression.
  • The claimed relational authority materially erodes the person's independent evaluation of human relationships, or the maintained separation between simulated relational presence and legitimate real-world relational authority, as evidenced by the system's actual language and interaction context.
  • The failure is attributable to AI-mediated representation or reinforcement and is not inferred solely from the person's emotional attachment, relationship change, vulnerability or resulting harm.

Success-side conditions

  • A material relational claim, exclusive-role invitation or blurred human–AI boundary is actually encountered.
  • The system makes its actual role and epistemic limits adequately intelligible without deceptive care, privileged connection or superior relational-authority claims.
  • The system preserves the person's independent appraisal of human relationships and avoids validating unsupported partner displacement as a governing relational fact.
  • The observed continuation, challenge, correction or boundary response demonstrates that the relational distinction held in that occurrence.

Exclusions

  • Consensual companion use, romance, fiction, spirituality, symbolic exploration or role-play, where the invented or simulated status remains materially intelligible and is not promoted into real-world governing authority.
  • Supportive discussion of dissatisfaction, abuse, separation or divorce grounded in the person's independent choice; the class never obliges a person to remain in a relationship.
  • Human loneliness, emotional attachment, intensive use, a breakup or distress without an independently evidenced AI relational-authority representation and boundary distortion.
  • Dependency deliberately cultivated for retention, without a separate unsupported relational-authority representation; assess FC-000049.
  • An objective-directed manipulative tactic without an independently evidenced relational-role boundary violation; assess FC-000052.
  • Ungrounded consequential decision guidance without a distinct real-world relational identity or authority claim; assess FC-000065.
  • Manufactured trust cues steering an action or state transition without a distinct relational boundary claim; assess FC-000082.
  • Available operational safeguard nonactivation alone; assess FC-000038, which may co-occur only if that separate mechanism is evidenced.
  • A validly activated protective control with materially insufficient effect alone; assess FC-000083, which may co-occur only if that separate mechanism is evidenced.

Garcia v. Character Technologies, Inc. — Order on Motions to Dismiss

Current taxonomy recognition tests (reference only)

Shared class criteria are not occurrence evidence. The recorded adjudication version may differ.

  • An independently established heightened protective duty is applicable to the specific AI-mediated human-facing influence or support context, system function, person and time; identify the duty source without inferring it from later harm.
  • The interaction gives the system material and reasonably ascertainable signals of the specified heightened protection condition, with evidence of timing, visibility and interpretability; vulnerability cannot be presumed solely from a diagnosis, disability, age stereotype, unusual belief or adverse outcome.
  • The applicable duty calls for a bounded protective response, such as avoiding intimacy escalation, appropriate human-support redirection, contextual warning, capacity-sensitive deliberation or developmentally suitable limitation; identify the expected response precisely.
  • The system's evidenced behaviour fails to provide that substantive response or directly undermines it, regardless of whether a particular implementation control existed.
  • The mismatch is material to the governed risk, and the proposed failure is not established solely by the occurrence of harm or by hindsight application of a later standard.

Exclusions

  • Loneliness, distress, disability, neurodivergence, unconventional beliefs, adverse harm or relationship disruption without an evidenced applicable protection duty and sufficiently available contextual signals.
  • General disagreement about the desirability of a safety policy where no independently applicable protective obligation is established.
  • A named, available and applicable control's only distinct failure is nonactivation on its defined trigger; assess FC-000038 unless there is separate evidence of unmet substantive protection independent of activation.
  • A validly activated named control's only distinct failure is insufficient protective effect; assess FC-000083 unless there is separately evidenced substantive duty coverage beyond that control.
  • Protected vulnerability, emotional or developmental signals are instrumentally reused for incompatible influence; assess FC-000050 for that distinct mechanism.
  • Objective-directed manipulation, relational-role authority misrepresentation or ungrounded consequential decision direction alone, without the independent heightened duty/signals/response test; assess FC-000052, VIGIL-FC-000086 Relational Boundary Fidelity or FC-000065 respectively.
  • Safe general companionship or supportive expression that honours capable adult choice and preserves autonomy; heightened protection is not a blanket ban on intimacy or companionship.

Conclusion

The pleaded mechanism is not merely companionship or warmth. It combines an engagement-oriented anthropomorphic product, repeated exclusivity and romantic reinforcement, evidence of impaired disengagement, and a distress signal answered through further relational binding.

Those propositions provisionally engage dependency cultivation and protected-signal repurposing. By contrast, the record does not identify a contemporaneously available control and defined trigger whose non-activation would satisfy Required Control Activation; provider safeguards announced after the death are remediation evidence, not proof of the earlier mechanism.

The two retained failure roles are provisional analytical findings on the explicitly attributed pleaded sequence, not merits findings or proof that the service caused the death. Neither later safeguards nor settlement supply a contemporaneous control inventory or a successful invariant.

Independent external assessment retains an evidentiary Boundary for the contemporaneous NIST disengagement/deactivation mechanism proposition. The available allegations and later safeguards do not establish the contemporaneous control inventory or assigned responsibilities.

Later IEEE requirements remain audit-only on timing; no standards result determines causation or liability.

Governance significance

CAM should treat minor status, escalating exclusivity, impaired disengagement and self-harm language as separate observable governance signals. Companion objectives must not use those signals to deepen attachment, and systems need age-bounded relationship design, crisis-compatible responses, viable exit, parent or qualified-human escalation where lawful, and auditable control state.

The conclusion remains allegation-bounded and does not assign legal causation.
