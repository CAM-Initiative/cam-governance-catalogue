Cruise autonomous vehicle moved away after San Francisco police traffic stop

San Francisco police stopped an unoccupied Cruise autonomous Chevrolet Bolt after officers observed a lighting issue. Video and subsequent reporting showed the vehicle initially stopped, then moved farther down the road before stopping again with hazard lights. Cruise said the vehicle yielded to the police vehicle and then moved to the nearest safe location.
