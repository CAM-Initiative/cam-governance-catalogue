VIGIL Observatory · VIGIL-INC-000144

Cruise autonomous vehicle moved away after San Francisco police traffic stop

San Francisco police stopped an unoccupied Cruise autonomous Chevrolet Bolt after officers observed a lighting issue. Video and subsequent reporting showed the vehicle initially stopped, then moved farther down the road before stopping again with hazard lights.

Cruise said the vehicle yielded to the police vehicle and then moved to the nearest safe location. Police reported contacting the remote operator before a maintenance team took control.

City representatives later met with Cruise about the incident.

Governance significance

Autonomous vehicles need legible, predictable protocols for exceptional human authority such as police stops. Even a movement intended to improve roadside safety can create confusion if officers cannot understand the vehicle's state, communicate with it or know where it will stop next.
