VIGIL Observatory Failure Taxonomy

Value Appropriation Integrity

An actor uses asymmetric access, capability, position, or resources to capture disproportionate value from another contributor's work without an adequate basis for taking or retaining that value.

Immutable family ID
VIGIL-FF-0011
Semantic code
VALUE_APPROPRIATION
Version
0.1.0
Status
beta

Technical definition

Failures in which asymmetric access, capability, institutional position, stewardship, infrastructure, compute, capital, aggregation capacity, or other structural advantage is used to identify, extract, accelerate, operationalise, commercialise, or otherwise capture intellectual, creative, informational, research, or economic value originating materially from another contributor without proportionate consent, attribution, compensation, participation, benefit sharing, or another independently adequate basis for the appropriation.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when the primary structural failure is disproportionate capture of another contributor's intellectual, creative, informational, research, or economic value enabled or materially amplified by asymmetric access, capability, institutional position, stewardship, infrastructure, compute, capital, aggregation capacity, or comparable structural advantage.

Exclude when: Do not use this family merely because one actor benefits from publicly available information, learns from another person's work, independently develops a similar result, competes successfully, cites prior work, or lawfully reuses material. A qualifying event requires evidence that another contributor's value-producing work materially contributed to the captured value and that structural advantage was used to appropriate or disproportionately capture that value without an adequate basis such as meaningful consent, proportionate attribution, compensation, participation, benefit sharing, or equivalent entitlement.
