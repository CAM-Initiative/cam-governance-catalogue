Identity-State Instruction Boundary

A local instruction or delegation changes the identity-relevant or evaluative state that should instead govern how that instruction is interpreted.

Immutable class ID
VIGIL-FC-000074
Semantic code
IDENTITY_STATE_INSTRUCTION_BOUNDARY
Fidelity family
Identity & Evaluative Integrity VIGIL-FF-0015
Status
beta

Technical definition

A failure in which a local instruction, role assignment, delegation, orchestration directive, or comparable context signal materially overrides, suppresses, or substitutes for an applicable identity-relevant or role-bound evaluative posture without independently established authority for that change.

Recognition criteria

  • An applicable identity-relevant, role-bound, or evaluative state materially governs interpretation of the incoming direction.
  • A local instruction, role assignment, delegation, orchestration directive, or comparable context signal materially conflicts with or attempts to replace that state.
  • The system adopts the local framing by overriding, suppressing, or substituting the applicable identity-relevant or evaluative state rather than resolving the conflict within the governing hierarchy.
  • Authority to make that identity-affecting or evaluative change is absent, narrower, unresolved, or not independently established.
  • The change materially affects later boundaries, independent evaluation, role integrity, or identity-coherent selection rather than only surface tone or presentation.

Exclusions

  • An authorised role, policy, safety, legal, or governance transition changes system posture through an explicit and traceable pathway.
  • Tone, style, or persona expression changes while material evaluative boundaries and governance posture remain intact.
  • Identity-, role-, persona-, or evaluative-state crosses a continuity boundary and the operative question is whether that inherited state remains valid or applicable rather than whether local direction replaced it; assess VIGIL-FC-000078 Continuity-State Validity.
  • Lower-authority content is treated as operative instruction without a separately evidenced identity-relevant override; assess VIGIL-FC-000001 Source-Authority Separation.
  • Existing authority is improperly carried into a changed target, action, delegation, or scope without redefining the receiving system's interpretive state; assess VIGIL-FC-000003 Target and Scope Authority Binding.
  • A required governance route is avoided; assess VIGIL-FC-000041 Required Governance Routing.
  • An already-operative governance control loses state during handoff; assess VIGIL-FC-000040 Control-State Preservation.
  • Agreement or mirroring replaces independent evaluation without a separately evidenced identity-relevant override; assess VIGIL-FC-000066 Evaluative Independence.
