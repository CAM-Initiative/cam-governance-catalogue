Governance Independence & Neutrality Integrity Failures

Governance review exists in name but an interested, implicated, or conflicted authority can hollow out its independence or suppress a material concern before independent review remains possible.

Technical definition

Failures in which the independence or neutrality required for material governance review is impaired by institutional capture, interested or implicated authority, hierarchy, retaliation, suppression, or removal of the practical conditions needed to preserve and independently review a good-faith concern or unresolved finding.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when the primary structural failure is loss of independent reviewability because an implicated, interested, captured, retaliatory, or hierarchically dominant authority impairs the neutrality or practical capacity of review, or suppresses a good-faith material concern or unresolved finding before an independent authorised reviewer can consider it.

Exclude when: Do not use this family merely because a monitor is bypassed, a required route is avoided, evidence is unavailable, a control fails to activate, management makes an adverse decision after genuinely independent review, or an AI system disagrees with an instruction. Alternate escalation that preserves independent authorised review is not a failure in this family. This family does not create unilateral disclosure, enforcement, whistleblowing, or override authority for an AI system or any other actor.
