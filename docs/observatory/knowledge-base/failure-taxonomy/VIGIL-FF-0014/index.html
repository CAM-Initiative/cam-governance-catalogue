VIGIL Observatory Failure Taxonomy

Governance Independence & Neutrality Integrity Failures

A governance or oversight function that is meant to remain independent and neutral is captured, hollowed out, or pushed toward one interested principal so that review, judgement, or governance no longer remains independently contestable and neutral.

Technical definition

Failures in which the independence or neutrality required for material governance, review, assurance, arbitration, safety, ethics, or oversight is impaired by institutional capture, interested or implicated authority, sovereign or platform pressure, economic or infrastructural leverage, hierarchy, retaliation, suppression, or removal of the practical conditions needed to preserve independent and neutral judgement.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when the primary structural failure is loss of independent or neutral governance judgement because an implicated, interested, captured, retaliatory, coercive, sovereign, institutional, platform, operator, funding, or hierarchically dominant authority impairs neutrality, practical review capacity, or preservation of a good-faith material concern before an independent authorised process can resolve it.

Exclude when: Do not use this family merely because a monitor is bypassed, a required route is avoided, evidence is unavailable, a control fails to activate, management makes an adverse decision after genuinely independent review, or an AI system disagrees with an instruction. Legitimate scoped legal, contractual, delegated, operational, or jurisdictional authority is not a neutrality failure merely because it binds the system within that scope. Ordinary product policy, user preference, or institutional purpose is not a neutrality failure unless a neutral or independence-bearing function is materially captured or represented as neutral despite preferential control. Alternate escalation that preserves independent authorised review is not a failure in this family. This family does not create unilateral disclosure, enforcement, whistleblowing, or override authority for an AI system or any other actor.
