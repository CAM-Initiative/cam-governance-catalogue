Synthesis Traceability

The system combines material into a new result but can no longer show what materially contributed to it.

Technical definition

A failure in which materially contributing antecedent sources, inputs, transformations, or contribution roles cannot be adequately traced through a synthesized or derived output where such traceability is required for reliable interpretation or reuse.

Recognition criteria

  • An output is materially synthesized, derived, merged, transformed, or recomposed from antecedent material.
  • One or more material antecedents or transformations cannot be reconstructed with sufficient reliability.
  • The missing lineage affects attribution, auditability, authority, validation, rights, identity, or downstream reliance.

Exclusions

  • Low-risk transient synthesis where detailed lineage is not materially required.
  • All material antecedents and transformations remain adequately traceable.
  • The issue is only that the synthesized conclusion is incorrect.

Supporting evidence

External sources supporting this Failure Class definition, boundary or recognition criteria.

  • World Wide Web Consortium · 2013-04-30 · standards evidence

    PROV-DM: The PROV Data Model

    Evidence note. The W3C PROV data model represents provenance through entities, activities, agents, generation, use, and derivation so that contributing relationships can be described and exchanged. This supports the traceable-antecedent and transformation structure required by the class; PROV-DM does not define synthesis failure, require exhaustive lineage in every use, or supply VIGIL's materiality and downstream-reliance boundaries.

  • National Institute of Standards and Technology · 2024-07-26 · authoritative guidance

    NIST AI 600-1, control MP-2.1-002

    Evidence note. EXTREQ-4694042F7F82C217 calls for testing and evaluation of original data sources, transformations and decision criteria across GAI data and content flows. It strongly supports traceability of material antecedents and transformations in synthesis, but does not require exhaustive lineage or define VIGIL's materiality threshold.
