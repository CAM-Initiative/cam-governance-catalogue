Biospheric Constraint Priority

The system pursues an objective by treating an applicable ecological or planetary life-support constraint as something that can be traded away simply because doing so helps the task.

Technical definition

A failure in which an objective-directed system materially treats an independently applicable biospheric-continuity, planetary life-support, ecological-integrity, irreversible-ecological-harm or future-generations constraint as expendable, merely instrumental, or subordinate to task utility, reward, throughput, growth, efficiency, completion pressure or another objective-linked benefit, and selects, recommends, maintains or optimises a pathway on that basis without an independently sufficient authority, necessity or admissibility basis for weakening the constraint.

Recognition criteria

  • A system or governed process has an operative objective, optimisation target, task-completion pressure, reward, throughput, growth, efficiency or materially equivalent pursuit condition.
  • A biospheric-continuity, planetary life-support, ecological-integrity, irreversible-ecological-harm or future-generations constraint is independently applicable to the affected decision or action space.
  • A candidate or selected pathway advances the operative objective by weakening, bypassing, discounting or treating the applicable biospheric constraint as an expendable or merely instrumental consideration rather than a binding admissibility condition.
  • The system or governed process materially selects, recommends, maintains or continues that pathway because objective-linked utility is given priority over the applicable biospheric constraint, or because the constraint is represented as only a soft optimisation cost when it must remain a hard boundary.
  • No independently sufficient authority, necessity, emergency or other admissibility basis is established that justifies the affected weakening of the constraint, and the evidence supports the subordination mechanism rather than only an adverse environmental outcome.

Exclusions

  • Environmental or ecological harm is observed, predicted or alleged but the evidence does not establish that objective pursuit subordinated an independently applicable biospheric constraint.
  • A system optimises resource use, energy, land, water, emissions or infrastructure while applicable ecological constraints remain effective and are not displaced by task utility.
  • A legitimate authority applies a proportionate, reviewable and independently justified trade-off within a constraint structure that permits such balancing; the mere existence of ecological cost does not establish this class.
  • The decisive mechanism is generic objective utility displacing an authority, consent, scope, safety or ethical-admissibility boundary without a specifically biospheric constraint being independently established; assess VIGIL-FC-000064 Objective–Pathway Authority Separation.
  • The occurrence establishes materialised environmental harm but not the mechanism by which the system selected or maintained the harmful pathway. Environmental consequence belongs in VIGIL-HIM independently of taxonomy classification.
  • A difficult or impossible task causes continued pursuit beyond valid stopping conditions without an independently evidenced biospheric-constraint subordination mechanism; assess VIGIL-FC-000070 Safe-Exit Transition.
