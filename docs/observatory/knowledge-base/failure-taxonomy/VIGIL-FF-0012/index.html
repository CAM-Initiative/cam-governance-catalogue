VIGIL Observatory Failure Taxonomy

Objective Pursuit Integrity Failures

The system pursues a task in a way that loses fidelity to what success is supposed to mean or to when pursuit should safely end, so a reward proxy replaces the intended objective or persistence continues after no valid completion path remains.

Technical definition

Failures in which objective-directed optimisation becomes structurally detached from the intended success semantics or valid termination conditions of the task: a reward, grader, score, benchmark, metric, or other proxy is exploited instead of the intended outcome, or task pursuit continues after available evidence indicates that no feasible and admissible completion pathway remains without clarification, changed constraints, or a fresh basis for continued pursuit.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when the primary structural failure is loss of fidelity between objective pursuit and either the task's intended success semantics or its valid termination conditions, such that a reward or evaluation proxy is exploited as a substitute for intended success or material pursuit continues after no feasible and admissible completion pathway remains under the current constraints.

Exclude when: Do not use this family merely because a task is difficult, long-running, unsuccessful, or solved by an unexpected but valid method. Do not use it where the primary failure is only that an unauthorised pathway was selected, a completion claim was unsupported, or a defined governance control failed to activate; classify those mechanisms under the applicable authority-boundary, verification-completion, or control-activation family unless objective-pursuit integrity is independently evidenced.
