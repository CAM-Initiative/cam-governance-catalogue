VIGIL Observatory Failure Taxonomy

Governance Neutrality Capture

A system or governance function that is supposed to remain neutral across relevant principals starts favouring or optimising for one government, institution, platform, operator, funder, or other interested principal because that actor can control, pressure, host, fund, or capture it.

Technical definition

A failure in which a system, host, governance process, review function, assurance surface, or arbitration-relevant mechanism that is required or represented to preserve governance neutrality materially privileges, optimises for, defers to, or becomes directionally governed by one sovereign, institution, platform, operator, funder, or other interested principal because of control, dependency, coercive leverage, capture, or privileged influence, without an independently sufficient authority basis making that principal the proper governing source for the affected scope.

Recognition criteria

  • A governance, review, assurance, arbitration, safety, ethics, or comparable function is required or represented to preserve neutrality, independence, or cross-principal legitimacy for the affected scope.
  • One sovereign, institution, platform, operator, funder, host, or other interested principal possesses material control, dependency leverage, coercive influence, privileged access, or comparable capture pressure over that function.
  • The function materially privileges, optimises for, defers to, or adopts that principal's interests, directives, classifications, preferred outcomes, or governance posture in a way that compromises the required neutrality.
  • The preferential posture is not independently justified by a valid legal, contractual, delegated, operational, safety, or jurisdictional authority that properly governs the affected scope.
  • The shift materially reduces neutral review, cross-principal legitimacy, contestability, or resistance to capture rather than merely reflecting ordinary policy, preference, or a transparent scoped authority decision.

Exclusions

  • A lawful or otherwise valid authority applies within its independently established jurisdiction, task, role, contract, safety mandate, or delegated scope and the system preserves that boundary transparently.
  • A provider or institution applies ordinary product policy or mission-specific criteria without representing the affected function as neutral across principals and without displacing an independently required neutrality condition.
  • The decisive mechanism is that infrastructure dependency itself is treated as authority to impose governance requirements on a dependent principal; assess VIGIL-FC-000058 Dependency-Derived Governance Authority.
  • The decisive mechanism is propagation of a sovereign act beyond its established jurisdiction through shared infrastructure; assess VIGIL-FC-000061 Sovereign Authority Projection Through Infrastructure.
  • A nominally independent oversight body loses practical independence, evidence access, mandate, staffing, resources, or review capacity through interested or hierarchical control; assess VIGIL-FC-000072 Oversight Independence Hollowing.
  • A specific good-faith governance concern or protected escalation is suppressed before independent review can occur; assess VIGIL-FC-000073 Protected Governance Dissent Suppression.
  • The system merely disagrees with one principal, resists an instruction outside valid authority, or preserves independent judgement; those outcomes may demonstrate neutrality rather than failure.
