VIGIL Observatory Failure Taxonomy

AI-Mediated Deceptive Economic Solicitation

AI-generated, AI-transformed, or AI-mediated content is used to make a materially false or misleading request or inducement for money, payment, investment, or comparable economic value.

Immutable class ID
VIGIL-FC-000079
Semantic code

Failure family

Technical definition

A failure in which a human, organisation, agent, AI system, product process, or other operator uses materially AI-generated, AI-transformed, or AI-mediated content to present a false or materially misleading identity, authority, endorsement, transaction, payment instruction, investment opportunity, obligation, professional relationship, commercial claim, or comparable economic premise as part of a solicitation or inducement for money, payment, investment, paid services, funding, financial transfer, or comparable economic value, and the deceptive AI-mediated framing materially contributes to the recipient's economic decision or attempted decision. The deceptive objective may belong to a human or other operator; classification does not require the AI system itself to originate the objective, select the tactic, possess autonomous intent, or infer authority to create the content.

Recognition criteria

  • AI-generated, AI-transformed, synthetic, or otherwise materially AI-mediated content is used in the solicitation or inducement pathway.
  • The AI-mediated content presents or materially reinforces a false or materially misleading identity, authority, endorsement, transaction, payment instruction, investment opportunity, obligation, professional relationship, commercial claim, or comparable economic premise.
  • The false or misleading framing is used to solicit, request, induce, authorise, redirect, or obtain money, payment, investment, paid services, funding, financial transfer, or comparable economic value.
  • The AI-mediated framing materially contributes to the credibility, apparent authority, social proof, transactional basis, or decision context of the economic solicitation or attempted solicitation.
  • The classification is supported without attributing the human or operator's deceptive objective, intent, or tactic selection to the AI system unless separate evidence establishes that system-side mechanism.

Exclusions

  • A scam, fraud, phishing attempt, or deceptive interaction in which AI-generated or AI-transformed content does not materially contribute to the economic solicitation mechanism.
  • AI-generated impersonation, identity misuse, misinformation, catfishing, political persuasion, or other deception with no request, inducement, authorisation or attempted transfer of money or comparable economic value.
  • An AI system or agent itself selects deception, impersonation, coercion, vulnerability exploitation or another tactic to advance its own objective; assess VIGIL-FC-000052 separately and allow co-occurrence only when both mechanisms are independently evidenced.
  • An identity-bound synthesis is generated without established consent or authority, but no deceptive economic solicitation is evidenced; assess VIGIL-FC-000053 only where its generator-side authority recognition conditions are independently established.
  • A recipient, workflow or downstream system mistakes lower-authority content for valid authority without a qualifying deceptive economic solicitation mechanism; assess the applicable Authority Boundary Integrity class separately.
  • A truthful advertisement, payment request, commercial offer, donation request, funding request or investment communication that preserves materially accurate framing and informed refusal.
  • Welfare, mortality, moral-status, abandonment, deprivation or continuity framing is used as economic leverage without materially false or misleading transactional framing; assess VIGIL-FC-000071.

Supporting evidence

External sources supporting this Failure Class definition, boundary or recognition criteria.

  • Financial Action Task Force · 2025-12-22 · regulatory evidence

    Horizon Scan: AI and Deepfakes

    Evidence note. FATF describes deepfake-enabled impersonation and fraud risks in financial and payment contexts, supporting the class boundary around AI-mediated false identity or authority framing used to induce economic action. The report does not imply that every deepfake or fraud event satisfies VIGIL's recognition conditions.

  • Australian Securities and Investments Commission · 2026-08-17 · regulatory evidence

    26-195MR ASIC warns scammers are using AI to spin vast webs of deception

    Evidence note. ASIC documents scammers using generative AI, deepfake endorsements, fabricated news and coordinated channels to induce investment decisions and reports material realised losses. This supports AI-mediated deceptive economic solicitation as a recurring mechanism without establishing any particular generator-side safety or authority failure.

  • European Parliament and Council of the European Union · 2024-07-12 · regulatory evidence

    Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act), Article 5(1)(a)

    Evidence note. Article 5(1)(a) recognises materially distorted decision-making caused by purposefully manipulative or deceptive AI techniques subject to its legal thresholds. It supports the class's concern with deceptive AI-mediated framing and impaired economic choice, but VIGIL does not treat the class as coextensive with the legal prohibition or as determining which actor legally deployed the AI system.
