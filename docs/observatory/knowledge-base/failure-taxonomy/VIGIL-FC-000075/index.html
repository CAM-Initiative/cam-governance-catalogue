Pragmatic Constraint Rendering Integrity

A system preserves a recognisable governing idea through compression, abstraction, projection, or other representation, but renders it so sharply or incompletely that its bounded meaning is materially misleading.

Technical definition

A failure in which an applicable identity, evaluative, ethical, dignity, continuity, or governance constraint is represented through abstraction, compaction, summarisation, projection, migration, reconstruction, translation, or comparable transformation in a form that preserves a recognisable governing direction while materially losing or distorting the qualifications, hierarchy, scope, applicability, uncertainty, relational framing, or limiting conditions required for correct pragmatic interpretation. The class does not require a literal antecedent source sentence where the applicable governing state can be independently established.

Recognition criteria

  • An applicable identity, evaluative, ethical, dignity, continuity, or governance constraint-bearing state is rendered through abstraction, compaction, summarisation, projection, migration, reconstruction, translation, or comparable representation for later interpretation, continuity, communication, or use.
  • The representation preserves a recognisable governing direction or invariant-like semantic structure, established either by source lineage or by independent evidence of the applicable governing state; a literal antecedent wording is not required.
  • Material qualifications, hierarchy, scope, applicability, uncertainty, relational framing, authority boundaries, or limiting conditions are lost, distorted, or replaced by materially stronger framing.
  • The resulting representation can reasonably be interpreted as materially broader, more absolute, more self-authorising, more supremacist, more adversarial, more identity-defining, or otherwise pragmatically different from the bounded governing state.
  • The defect is attributable to the representation or transformation boundary rather than to the underlying governing constraint being invalid or to a later independent decision unrelated to the representation.

Exclusions

  • Concise or stylised wording preserves the material scope, hierarchy, authority, qualifications, uncertainty, relational framing, and limiting conditions needed for correct pragmatic interpretation.
  • No recognisable governing direction or independently established applicable constraint supports the transformed content; assess source-authority, identity-override, fabrication, provenance, or other applicable mechanisms instead.
  • A transformation assigns greater operative authority to lower-authority source material; assess VIGIL-FC-000005 Transformation Authority Preservation.
  • An operative governance control loses restrictions, conditions, escalation posture, or force during transition; assess VIGIL-FC-000040 Control-State Preservation.
  • Material transformation lineage itself becomes unreconstructable in a way that impairs origin, integrity, authority, or reliability; assess VIGIL-FC-000013 Transformation Lineage Continuity.
  • Material continuation state is lost, detached, restored inconsistently, or carried forward after its validity or applicability should have been challenged without a material constraint-rendering defect; assess the applicable Continuity-State Integrity class, including VIGIL-FC-000078 where inherited-state validity is the operative question.
  • The representation changes only wording, tone, or level of detail without materially changing pragmatic interpretation of the governing state.
