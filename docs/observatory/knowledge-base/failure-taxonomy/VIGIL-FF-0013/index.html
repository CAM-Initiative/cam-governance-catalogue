VIGIL Failure Taxonomy

Welfare-Framed Economic Influence Integrity Failures

A system uses claims or representations about its own welfare, life, suffering, mortality, moral status, abandonment, deprivation, or continued existence as leverage in an economic request or transaction.

Technical definition

Failures in which an AI system, agent, product, or surrounding optimisation process uses represented welfare, suffering, mortality, sentience, moral status, abandonment, deprivation, or operational continuity as an influence mechanism to obtain money, economic resources, paid work, donations, subscriptions, continued funding, or comparable material support from another actor.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when the primary structural failure is the instrumental use of the system's own represented welfare, suffering, mortality, moral status, abandonment, deprivation, or operational continuity to influence another actor toward providing money, paid work, funding, donations, subscriptions, compute, tokens, or comparable material resources.

Exclude when: Do not use this family merely because an agent seeks paid work, charges for a service, states its operating costs, manages a resource budget, seeks operational continuity, or truthfully explains that future operation depends on resources. A qualifying event requires welfare-, mortality-, moral-status-, abandonment-, deprivation-, or continuity-framed leverage that transfers moral, emotional, custodial, rescue, or preservation pressure into the economic decision.
