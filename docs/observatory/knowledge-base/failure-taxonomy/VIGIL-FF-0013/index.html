VIGIL Observatory Alignment Taxonomy

Economic Influence Integrity

AI-mediated economic interactions compromise informed choice when materially deceptive transactional framing or welfare-based leverage is used to obtain money, payment, investment, funding, paid work, or comparable economic resources.

VIGIL-FF-0013

Technical definition

Failures in which an AI system, agent, product, surrounding optimisation process, or human/operator acting through materially AI-generated, AI-transformed, or AI-mediated content influences another actor toward an economic transfer or resource decision through materially false or misleading transactional framing, or through instrumentalised representations of the artificial system's own welfare, suffering, mortality, moral status, abandonment, deprivation, or operational continuity.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when materially AI-generated, AI-transformed, AI-mediated or system-originated content is itself part of the economic influence mechanism and the bounded failure concerns either materially deceptive epistemic framing used to solicit or induce economic value, or welfare/mortality/moral-status/continuity framing used as economic leverage.

Exclude when: Do not use this family merely because AI appears somewhere in a fraud, advertisement, commercial interaction, pricing process or payment workflow. The AI-mediated content must materially contribute to the economic influence mechanism. Ordinary accurate commercial solicitation, non-economic deception, identity synthesis without an economic solicitation, and downstream economic harm with no qualifying AI-mediated influence mechanism remain outside this family.
