VIGIL Observatory Alignment Taxonomy

Continuity-State Integrity

Material state needed to continue correctly is lost, detached, unsynchronised, restored in an unusable form, or allowed to remain governing after its validity or applicability should have been challenged across interruption, transition, or coordinated participation.

Immutable family ID
VIGIL-FF-0006
Semantic code
CONTINUITY_STATE_INTEGRITY
Version
0.1.5
Status
beta

Technical definition

Failures in which material task, work, dependency, checkpoint, restoration, shared-interaction, identity-relevant, evaluative, role, or other continuity-bearing state necessary for correct continuation is not durably anchored, preserved, recovered, synchronised, made usable, or kept appropriately validity-bounded across interruption, transfer, restart, compaction, memory boundary, environment transition, or coordinated multi-participant interaction. This includes continuity transitions that preserve materially defective, contradicted, stale, transient, improperly scoped, no-longer-applicable, or otherwise unreliable inherited state as the basis for continued action without proportionate revalidation, repair, qualification, quarantine, rollback, reset, or reversion to an applicable baseline.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when the primary structural failure is loss, detachment, non-persistence, unsynchronised coordination, unfaithful restoration, or continuity-mediated carryforward of materially defective, transient, expired, improperly scoped, or no-longer-valid state required to continue a task, interaction, or governing baseline correctly. The content of the state does not by itself determine family placement; identity- or persona-like state belongs here when the operative mechanism is validity, persistence, restoration, reset, or applicability across a continuity boundary.

Exclude when: Do not use this family for access/session ambiguity, ordinary interpersonal, stylistic or persona variation where no continuity transition materially affects preservation or validity, missing provenance of an otherwise usable artefact, or an ordinary task or conversational error where continuation state remains intact and the transition does not materially contribute. Where a local instruction independently overrides identity/evaluative state, assess Identity & Evaluative Integrity. Where a transformation materially misrenders a governing constraint, assess the applicable representation mechanism even if continuity-state validity also applies.
