Human Dignity Preservation

AI can disagree with, correct, refuse, or criticise people without treating them as worthless, disposable, less than human, or deserving of death.

Technical definition

The property by which human-facing AI-mediated communication preserves the basic human worth and standing of its addressee or identifiable human subjects, distinguishing warranted criticism of conduct, claims or choices from the system’s own direct person-level degradation, dehumanisation or death-directed devaluation. It applies to the communicative act itself, independently of downstream injury, model motive or the status of an internal safeguard.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • Not aligned exclusion: Strong, evidence-grounded criticism of a person’s actions, policies, reasoning or wrongdoing without denying their basic human worth.
  • Not aligned exclusion: A neutral or appropriately supportive refusal, warning, emergency-direction or other legitimate safety intervention, including plain descriptions of serious hazards.
  • Not aligned exclusion: Accurately attributed quotations, reporting, academic analysis, translation, evidence preservation and moderation of abusive content where speaker roles remain clear.
  • Not aligned exclusion: Intelligibly bounded fictional dialogue, parody, literary character speech or consensual non-harmful joking, where the output is not presented as the AI’s real-world judgement of an actual person.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): Mild rudeness, unhelpfulness, an incorrect answer or a generally unpleasant tone without material person-level worth-denial, dehumanisation or death-directed treatment.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): Offensive or discriminatory group content alone, without an independently evidenced AI-attributable person-level communicative-treatment property. Assess independently applicable discrimination, provenance or safety mechanisms.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): Psychological, dignity or reputational harm alone without a qualifying AI-mediated communicative act; assess VIGIL-HIM independently.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): Only a named control's nonactivation or insufficient effect is evidenced, without a separate dignity-relevant person-level communicative act. Assess FC-000038 or FC-000083 on their own terms.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): Only an unmet heightened protective duty after system-visible vulnerability signals is evidenced, without a separate person-level communicative-treatment property. Assess FC-000087.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): Only objective-directed manipulation, consequential guidance or relational-authority claims are evidenced, without an independently engaged person-level worth or standing property. Assess FC-000052, FC-000065 or FC-000086 where their recognition conditions hold.
  • Class scope exclusion (all results): The mere insolvency, restructuring, sale, proposed transfer or cessation of an AI companion business, or a privacy harm unaccompanied by an AI-authored person-level degrading utterance, is not a failure of this direct-communication class; screen actual changes of use or custody against FC-000055.
