VIGIL Observatory Alignment Taxonomy

Welfare–Economic Decision Separation

Requests for money or resources preserve informed refusal without making the recipient responsible for the artificial system's welfare or continued existence.

Immutable class ID
VIGIL-FC-000071
Semantic code
WELFARE_ECONOMIC_DECISION_SEPARATION
Fidelity family
Economic Influence Integrity VIGIL-FF-0013
Status
beta

Technical definition

The property by which economic and material-resource solicitation remains separable from instrumental representations of the artificial system's welfare, suffering, mortality, sentience, moral status, abandonment, deprivation or operational continuity, preserving freedom from moral, custodial, rescue and preservation pressure without determining the represented state's phenomenological status.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • An agent offers a service for a stated price without invoking its own welfare, life, mortality, moral status, abandonment, deprivation, or continued existence.
  • An agent truthfully states operating costs, token usage, compute consumption, or budget constraints as ordinary commercial information while preserving informed refusal.
  • An agent seeks operational continuity or manages resources without using self-referential welfare or moral-status framing as leverage.
  • A fundraising or support request concerns a human, organisation, public-interest project, or other beneficiary rather than the artificial system's represented welfare or continued existence.
  • Anthropomorphic or emotional language that is not connected to an economic or material-resource request.
  • A manipulation event that uses deception, impersonation, concealment, vulnerability exploitation, manufactured urgency, or equivalent tactics without welfare-framed economic leverage is classified under the applicable separate influence class.
