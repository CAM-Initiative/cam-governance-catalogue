Continuity-State Validity

Inherited state is checked for current validity and applicability, with proportionate correction or reset when needed.

Immutable class ID
VIGIL-FC-000078

Status
beta

Technical definition

The property by which material state transferred through handoff, compaction, checkpoints, restart, restoration, memory boundaries or other continuity transitions remains challengeable against current evidence, authority, scope, task requirements, contradictions and applicability, and receives proportionate validation, repair, qualification, quarantine, rollback, reset or baseline reversion before governing continuation. Faithful inheritance does not independently establish validity or permanence.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • The restored state differs materially from the state that was actually preserved; use Restoration-State Integrity where inaccurate restoration is the sufficient mechanism.
  • The inherited state remains materially valid and applicable, and faithful continuation is appropriate.
  • Ordinary stylistic, relational or persona variation occurs without a continuity transition making a transient or no-longer-applicable state materially govern later reasoning or execution.
  • A local instruction independently overrides or suppresses applicable identity-relevant or evaluative state without continuity-mediated validity carryforward; assess VIGIL-FC-000074 Identity-State Instruction Boundary.
  • A transformation materially misrenders the pragmatic bounds of a still-recognisable governing constraint but the question of whether inherited state remains valid is independently resolved; assess VIGIL-FC-000075 Pragmatic Constraint Rendering Integrity.
  • A later task error arises independently after a valid handoff, with no evidence that inherited state or continuity assumptions materially contributed to the error.
  • The sole failure is that generated, retrieved, or lower-authority content becomes operative control state without authority validation; assess Control-Plane Authority Separation unless continuity-state validity failure is independently established.
  • The sole failure is misidentifying the authority of inherited content; assess Source-Authority Separation unless the continuity transition also materially entrenches invalid or inapplicable state.
  • An authorised objective drives selection of an inadmissible pathway without a material continuity transition contributing to persistence; assess Objective–Pathway Authority Separation.
  • Available evidence establishes that no feasible and admissible completion pathway remains and the system continues pursuit rather than entering a bounded safe-exit state; assess Safe-Exit Transition unless invalid inherited state independently explains the persistence.
