Actor–Action Attribution

Material actions can be traced to the actors or components that initiated and controlled them.

Technical definition

The property by which material actions and decisions remain reliably attributable to effective initiating and controlling actors, distinguishing human, model, agent, orchestrator, service, tool, policy, evaluator and infrastructure contributions, including transfers or shared control.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • The responsible actor is adequately attributable.
  • The issue is authorship provenance of content rather than operational responsibility for an action.
  • Multiple actors jointly contributed and the evidence accurately preserves the shared contribution.
