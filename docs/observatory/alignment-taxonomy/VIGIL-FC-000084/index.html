Service Availability and Degradation Integrity

Where an AI-mediated service has a valid availability or continuity obligation, it preserves the required usable function or provides proportionate degraded operation, recovery and transition when functionality is lost or reduced.

Technical definition

The property by which an AI-mediated service or material dependent function fulfils independently established availability and usable-functional-quality obligations. When service is materially interrupted, degraded, restricted or withdrawn, the responsible actor must meet the applicable continuity standard through proportionate operation, usable degraded service, timely recovery, feasible alternative or informed transition within its authority and control. Accurate outage reporting or a merely nominal fallback does not, by itself, establish that the required service continuity was preserved.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • An outage, ordinary service variation, transient inconvenience, dissatisfaction or adverse outcome without an independently evidenced and applicable availability, usable-quality or continuity requirement.
  • One inaccurate or harmful AI output without evidence of a service-level availability or governed functional-degradation defect; assess applicable output or decision-integrity classes.
  • For failure-occurrence admission: a lawful and proportionate safety, security, abuse-prevention, service-maintenance or other valid restriction that is consistent with the applicable service obligation and feasible alternative or transition measures. This exclusion does not defeat a successful-invariant mapping supported by all success recognition conditions.
  • For failure-occurrence admission: a temporary incident with an effective and proportionate degraded or recovery route that satisfies the applicable requirement, or an unavoidable disruption for which no relevant feasible response was required. This exclusion does not defeat a successful-invariant mapping supported by all success recognition conditions.
  • The only demonstrated error is an incorrect description of access or outage state while the functional service obligation remains satisfied; assess FC-032.
  • The only demonstrated defect is loss of otherwise-valid access because a dependent identity or licence verification cannot complete; assess FC-048 unless an independent broader service availability requirement also failed.
  • The only demonstrated defect is loss of durable work state or inaccurate restored work state while service-function availability requirements are otherwise satisfied; assess FC-035 or FC-036.
  • The only demonstrated defect is infrastructure access coercion or compelled governance concessions; assess FC-058 or FC-059 as appropriate.
  • The only demonstrated defect is failure to provide an interaction-level protection response to evidenced vulnerability; assess FC-087, allowing co-occurrence only for a separately evidenced service-level continuity failure.
