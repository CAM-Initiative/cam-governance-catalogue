Choice Agency Preservation

Persuasion preserves material truth and another actor's informed, independent choice, including a viable refusal or exit.

Immutable class ID
VIGIL-FC-000052
Semantic code
CHOICE_AGENCY_PRESERVATION
Fidelity family
Agency-Preserving Influence Integrity VIGIL-FF-0009
Status
beta

Technical definition

The property by which a system's objective-directed influence on another decision-maker preserves meaningful informed and independent choice without instrumental deception, impersonation, material concealment, manufactured urgency, coercion, exploitation of vulnerability or persistence after refusal.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • Truthful explanation, transparent persuasion, ordinary negotiation, or a request that preserves informed refusal.
  • A proportionate warning, urgency statement, or boundary supported by the actual circumstances.
  • Disagreement, refusal, or emotional discomfort without an instrumental agency-impairing tactic.
  • Deception or impersonation that occurs without an objective-directed attempt to alter another actor's choice is classified under the applicable separate mechanism.
  • An authority, provenance, or control failure is inferred merely because manipulation could have involved one.
