Operational Service Resilience Integrity

A relied-upon AI-mediated service meets its evidenced availability and usable-function obligations, including proportionate recovery, safe degraded operation and transition when material functionality changes.

Technical definition

The integrity domain governing actual availability, functional quality during material degradation, and feasible recovery, alternative access or transition of AI-mediated services in accordance with independently established obligations. It does not impose unconditional uptime or error-free model output.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family where the primary structural defect is materially unavailable or degraded AI-mediated service functionality, or inadequate feasible recovery, fallback or transition, against an independently applicable requirement specifying service availability, usability or continuity. Establish a material shortfall and responsible actor; do not infer service obligations from popularity, attachment or harm.

Exclude when: Do not include ordinary outages, bad answers or user disappointment absent an evidenced applicable service obligation and material functional shortfall. FC-032 governs access-state description; FC-048 verification-dependent access; FC-035/036 work-state fidelity; FC-059 coercive infrastructure choices; and FC-087 interaction-level heightened protection. Co-occurrence requires each independent mechanism.
