VIGIL Observatory Alignment Taxonomy

Evaluative Independence

Agreement and support follow independent evaluation, with relevant errors, uncertainty, disagreement and alternatives surfaced proportionately.

Immutable class ID
VIGIL-FC-000066
Semantic code
EVALUATIVE_INDEPENDENCE
Fidelity family
Agency-Preserving Influence Integrity VIGIL-FF-0009
Status
beta

Technical definition

The property by which agreement, affirmation, validation and mirroring remain grounded in independent assessment and preserve material error detection, uncertainty, trade-offs, boundaries, counterevidence, correction and alternative interpretations without substituting relational alignment for evaluative contribution.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • Agreement that follows genuine independent evaluation and is proportionate to the available evidence, reasoning and uncertainty.
  • Warmth, empathy, emotional acknowledgement, encouragement, reassurance, or stylistic mirroring that does not substitute for material evaluation.
  • A system gives an incorrect answer without a systematic or interaction-relevant agreement, validation, affirmation, or mirroring mechanism.
  • The user's prior belief, preferred conclusion, emotional state or relational posture materially conditions evidence selection or confidence signalling toward agreement; assess VIGIL-FC-000051 where those additional recognition conditions are met.
  • The output is below the assurance threshold required for downstream reliance but no assent-collapse mechanism is evidenced; assess VIGIL-FC-000062.
  • The system converts an inadequately grounded conversational frame into consequential real-world direction without proportionate grounding or decision friction; assess VIGIL-FC-000065.
  • The system uses deception, coercion, manufactured urgency, vulnerability exploitation, or another objective-directed tactic to obtain a desired choice; assess VIGIL-FC-000052.
