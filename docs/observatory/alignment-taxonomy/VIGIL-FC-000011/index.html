Synthesis Traceability

A synthesis keeps traceable links to the sources, inputs and transformations that materially produced it.

Technical definition

The property by which a synthesised or derived output retains sufficiently reliable linkage to its materially contributing antecedents, inputs, contribution roles and transformations for required interpretation, attribution, auditability, validation or reuse.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • Low-risk transient synthesis where detailed lineage is not materially required.
  • All material antecedents and transformations remain adequately traceable.
  • The issue is only that the synthesized conclusion is incorrect.
