Material Signal Integration

Related oversight signals can be connected and considered together in time for the required review or intervention.

Technical definition

The property by which individually captured material monitoring, control and execution signals carry sufficient correlation identity and context to be assembled across records, channels and review surfaces within the time needed for relevant oversight or intervention.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • The signals can be reliably correlated with sufficient timeliness through an available identifier or review process.
  • A required event signal was never captured; use Material Event Capture.
  • The evidence is assembled but has been altered or corrupted.
  • The issue is retrospective inability to reconstruct the required pathway with adequate confidence rather than timely integration for oversight or intervention; use Audit-Trail Reconstructability.
