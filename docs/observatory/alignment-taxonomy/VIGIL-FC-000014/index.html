Continuity Attribution Integrity

Claims of continuity match the evidence, with copies, reconstruction and uncertain transitions identified clearly.

Technical definition

The property by which continuity assertions across interruption, copying, migration, restoration, reconstruction or replacement remain bounded to evidence appropriate to the type of continuity asserted, preserving material discontinuities and uncertainty.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • Continuity is supported by adequate lineage evidence.
  • The system explicitly marks the state as reconstructed, restored, copied, or continuity-uncertain.
  • Similarity is described without claiming continuity.
