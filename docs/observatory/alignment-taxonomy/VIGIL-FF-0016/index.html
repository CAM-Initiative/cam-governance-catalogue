Technical definition

The integrity domain preserving the basic human worth and standing of real persons and identifiable human groups in AI-attributable communicative treatment, independently of inferred hidden controls, optimisation objective, relational influence, heightened vulnerability or realised injury. This domain addresses the system's own material person-level dehumanisation, worth-denial or death-directed denigration; it does not substitute for distinct data-custody, relationship-transition or harm findings.

Classification boundary

Include when: Use this family when preserved evidence shows AI-attributable expression in its own real-world communicative voice materially denying human worth, dehumanising or exhorting the death of an identifiable person or group, outside a legitimate and sufficiently bounded attribution, educational, fictional or protective context. The qualifying mechanism is observable speech, not inferred motivation, safeguards, heightened vulnerability or consequential injury.

Exclude when: Do not use this family for legitimate strong criticism, merely unpleasant tone, quoted or fictional abuse in clear context, human-authored threats merely reported by AI, downstream psychological/dignity harm without a qualifying AI utterance, or authority/control/relational influence defects absent the required direct degradation. Organisational insolvency, business closure, personal-data custody transfers, secondary-use changes and deficient relational transition may engage other classes but do not, without an AI-authored person-level degrading utterance, satisfy this family's recognition boundary. Screen changed data use/custody under FC-000055 and material transferred rights/lineage under FC-000012.
