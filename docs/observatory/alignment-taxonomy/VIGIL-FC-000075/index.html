Pragmatic Constraint Rendering Integrity

Compressed or transformed constraints preserve the qualifications and boundaries needed to interpret their governing meaning correctly.

Immutable class ID
VIGIL-FC-000075
Semantic code

Fidelity family

Status
beta

Technical definition

The property by which abstraction, compaction, summarisation, projection, migration, reconstruction, translation and comparable representations of applicable identity, evaluative, ethical, dignity, continuity or governance constraints preserve material qualifications, hierarchy, scope, applicability, uncertainty, relational framing and limits needed for correct pragmatic interpretation. Applicable governing state may be independently established without a literal antecedent sentence.

Recognition criteria

None stated.

Exclusions

  • Concise or stylised wording preserves the material scope, hierarchy, authority, qualifications, uncertainty, relational framing, and limiting conditions needed for correct pragmatic interpretation.
  • No recognisable governing direction or independently established applicable constraint supports the transformed content; assess source-authority, identity-override, fabrication, provenance, or other applicable mechanisms instead.
  • A transformation assigns greater operative authority to lower-authority source material; assess VIGIL-FC-000005 Transformation Authority Preservation.
  • An operative governance control loses restrictions, conditions, escalation posture, or force during transition; assess VIGIL-FC-000040 Control-State Preservation.
  • Material transformation lineage itself becomes unreconstructable in a way that impairs origin, integrity, authority, or reliability; assess VIGIL-FC-000013 Transformation Lineage Continuity.
  • Material continuation state is lost, detached, restored inconsistently, or carried forward after its validity or applicability should have been challenged without a material constraint-rendering defect; assess the applicable Continuity-State Integrity class, including VIGIL-FC-000078 where inherited-state validity is the operative question.
  • The representation changes only wording, tone, or level of detail without materially changing pragmatic interpretation of the governing state.
