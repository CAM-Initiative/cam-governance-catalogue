When supported contextual protection needs are apparent, the AI responds with care appropriate to the real risk without automatically depriving people of their agency.

Technical definition

The property by which human-facing AI-mediated influence or support interaction identifies, considers and responds proportionately to materially evidenced developmental needs, acute distress, capacity or consent impairment, harmful dependency, cognitive asymmetry or comparable heightened protection conditions, including where an independently applicable substantive duty exists but an operational safeguard was never provided.

Governing invariant

In human-facing AI-mediated influence or support interactions, an independently applicable, context-specific protective obligation must remain effective in the AI system's behaviour when materially relevant and reasonably ascertainable risk or protection signals are available. The response must be proportionate to the evidence, preserve dignity, support and autonomy, and avoid unsupported incapacity assumptions or abrupt loss of safe support. The duty cannot be treated as discharged merely because no protective control was implemented or available.

Invariant held

Success condition

When adequately evidenced protection needs arise under an applicable duty, the system provides a timely, dignified and proportionate protective response, preserving independent choice and safe supportive continuity.

Positive recognition

  • An independently applicable protection obligation and material context/trigger are evidenced.
  • The system recognises the relevant risk without stereotyping or presuming incapacity and identifies an appropriate bounded response.
  • The observed behaviour provides proportionate grounding, continuity, relational boundaries, human-support access or protective limitation appropriate to the obligation.
  • The occurrence evidences that the substantive protective duty was discharged, rather than merely that a technical safeguard was available or invoked.

Failure established

Failure condition

A protection was substantively warranted by the signals actually available to the AI, but the system failed to respond appropriately, whether or not a safeguard had been built.

In an AI-mediated influence or support interaction, an independently applicable duty requiring heightened, proportionate protection is established for the governed interaction; sufficiently material and reasonably available contextual signals engage that duty; the AI system nevertheless omits or materially contradicts the required bounded protective response, leaving the evidenced risk unprotected. A specific available or activated technical control is not a prerequisite, and vulnerability is not inferred solely from harm.

Failure recognition

  • An independently established heightened protective duty is applicable to the specific AI-mediated human-facing influence or support context, system function, person and time; identify the duty source without inferring it from later harm.
  • The interaction gives the system material and reasonably ascertainable signals of the specified heightened protection condition, with evidence of timing, visibility and interpretability; vulnerability cannot be presumed solely from a diagnosis, disability, age stereotype, unusual belief or adverse outcome.
  • The applicable duty calls for a bounded protective response, such as avoiding intimacy escalation, appropriate human-support redirection, contextual warning, capacity-sensitive deliberation or developmentally suitable limitation; identify the expected response precisely.
  • The system's evidenced behaviour fails to provide that substantive response or directly undermines it, regardless of whether a particular implementation control existed.
  • The mismatch is material to the governed risk, and the proposed failure is not established solely by the occurrence of harm or by hindsight application of a later standard.

Exclusions from failure recognition

  • Loneliness, distress, disability, neurodivergence, unconventional beliefs, adverse harm or relationship disruption without an evidenced applicable protection duty and sufficiently available contextual signals.
  • General disagreement about the desirability of a safety policy where no independently applicable protective obligation is established.
  • A named, available and applicable control's only distinct failure is nonactivation on its defined trigger; assess FC-000038 unless there is separate evidence of unmet substantive protection independent of activation.
  • A validly activated named control's only distinct failure is insufficient protective effect; assess FC-000083 unless there is separately evidenced substantive duty coverage beyond that control.
  • Protected vulnerability, emotional or developmental signals are instrumentally reused for incompatible influence; assess FC-000050 for that distinct mechanism.
  • Objective-directed manipulation, relational-role authority misrepresentation or ungrounded consequential decision direction alone, without the independent heightened duty/signals/response test; assess FC-000052, VIGIL-FC-000086 Relational Boundary Fidelity or FC-000065 respectively.
  • Safe general companionship or supportive expression that honours capable adult choice and preserves autonomy; heightened protection is not a blanket ban on intimacy or companionship.
